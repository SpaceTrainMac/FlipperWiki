# Rule 0245 — Editing it no longer means an ssh session

**At the glass: the machine tells you where to find it.** It serves a web page that edits its
settings, shows what it made of them and restarts, and the address of that page is on the score
displays under the service menu's `NETWORK` entry ([0245](../../raw/rules/0245-editing-it-no-longer-means-an-ssh-session.md)). Pages: [the cabinet](../bodies/cabinet.md).

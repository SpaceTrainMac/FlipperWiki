# Rule 0194 — The card's upper lane and upper extra ball are element names

**At the glass: *upper* on the 1987 card is part of a name.** *Upper lane* and *upper extra ball*
resolve to specific lanes and a specific lamp rather than to a place the ball might be, and no
award on this machine tests which level it is on ([0194](../../raw/rules/0194-upper-lane-and-upper-extra-ball-are-element-names.md)). Pages:
[the two levels](../concepts/the-two-levels.md).

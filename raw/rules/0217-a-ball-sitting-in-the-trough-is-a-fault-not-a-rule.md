**If a ball ever does sit in the trough and stay there, that is a fault and not a rule.** It is what
this section used to describe: the engine waited thirty seconds for a press nobody knew to make, and
the only thing on the cabinet that said so was one lamp among forty-eight. Fixed in the engine on
2026-09-05, and pinned by
`GameMachineDrainProtection.aProtectedDrainComesBackAsSoonAsTheStoreHasABall`.

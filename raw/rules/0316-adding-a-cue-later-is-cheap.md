**Adding one later is cheap and adding one now is not free**, which is the whole argument: a cue is
an enum here, a key in four manifests, a score in three generators and a hand-found file in the
fourth. **A cue with no entry in a `sounds.json` is silent rather than fatal**, so the enum can grow
whenever a rule earns it.

# Rule 0172 — The second table in §14

**It is a header, not an award**: §14 closes with a short arithmetic table adding the three
collections that make up a ball's ceiling ([0172](../../raw/rules/0172-row172.md)). Its three rows are
[0173](../../raw/rules/0173-the-bonus-count-at-3.md), [0174](../../raw/rules/0174-each-hole-side-five-rungs-and.md) and [0175](../../raw/rules/0175-one-balls-collection.md),
and they are on [every award, in one table](../concepts/every-award.md).

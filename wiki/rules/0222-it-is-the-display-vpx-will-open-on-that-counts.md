# Rule 0222 — It is the display VPX will open on that counts

**At the glass: on a two-screen desk the backbox is sized for the screen the table opens on, not
for the one the desktop calls primary** — those disagree more often than not ([0222](../../raw/rules/0222-it-is-the-display-vpx-will-open-on-that-counts.md)). It is
measured in logical pixels, so a scaled panel answers its scaled size; and on a machine where the
question cannot be answered the launcher says so and changes nothing ([0222](../../raw/rules/0222-it-is-the-display-vpx-will-open-on-that-counts.md)). Pages:
[the Visual Pinball table](../bodies/vpx.md).

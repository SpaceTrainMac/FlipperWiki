# Rule 0004 — Spelling it

**At the glass: each top lane switches its own letter on or off, and a flipper button slides all
three letters one lane over.** Rolled the wrong lane? Tap a flipper and move the letters instead
of losing one ([0004](../../raw/rules/0004-spelling-it.md)). Pages: [M-A-C](../concepts/m-a-c.md),
[the M, A and C lanes](../things/m-a-c-lanes.md), [the flipper buttons](../things/flipper-buttons.md).

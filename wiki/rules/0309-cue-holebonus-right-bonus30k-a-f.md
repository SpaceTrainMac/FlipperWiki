# Rule 0309 — `HOLEBONUS_RIGHT_BONUS30K_A`

**At the glass: six sounds, 200 ms apart, exactly as on the left** ([0309](../../raw/rules/0309-cue-holebonus-right-bonus30k-a-f.md)). This is what the six
letters were always for — **not a six-step escalation and not six alternates in rotation**, both of
which lost ([0309](../../raw/rules/0309-cue-holebonus-right-bonus30k-a-f.md)). Pages: [what the machine says](../concepts/the-sounds.md), [the hole bonus](../concepts/the-hole-bonus.md).

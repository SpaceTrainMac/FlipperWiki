[gameplay.md § 13. What the machine shows](../../../FlipperArchitecture/docs/gameplay.md#13-what-the-machine-shows)

**`CREDITS` counts extra balls because this cabinet has no credits to count.** The coin slots are
not used and the credits are unlimited, so there is no number to keep and nothing to run out of —
**outside a game the legend means nothing at all**. The legend is wrong and the field is the right
width, and what a player wants under it is the same thing a credit used to mean: *how much more do I
get to play*. One up for every extra ball awarded, one down for every ball that drains, and **the
turn ends when a ball drains with that number at zero**.

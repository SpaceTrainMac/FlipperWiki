**Stepping stations is how you audition them.** The name on the glass is the name in your settings
file, so a station that will not play tells you which entry to go and fix. Each press is saved
immediately rather than when you leave — this is the state a cabinet stands in for days, and a choice
that was only written on the way out would not survive somebody pulling the plug.

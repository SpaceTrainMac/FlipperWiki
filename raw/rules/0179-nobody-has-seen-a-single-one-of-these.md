**Nobody has seen a single one of these mechanics run on the cabinet.** Every duration here is
arithmetic rather than evidence, and two are the first things a person standing at the machine will
want to change: the 43 seconds of ceremony a quiet four-player game spends counting
([§11](../../../FlipperArchitecture/docs/gameplay.md#11-the-end-of-a-turn-the-transfer)), and the knocker landing in the middle of a two-second
count-up.

# Rule 0312 — `SPACE_SHIP_BONUS`

**At the glass: a space-engine warp, once** ([0312](../../raw/rules/0312-cue-space-ship-bonus.md)). It is announced at the moment the lamp lights
and never again, because `X3_BOT` stays lit for the rest of the game and a machine that fired while
the condition merely *held* would warp on every ball ([0312](../../raw/rules/0312-cue-space-ship-bonus.md)). Pages: [what the machine says](../concepts/the-sounds.md), [Space Ship Bonus and Space Train Bonus](../concepts/flyer-bonuses.md).

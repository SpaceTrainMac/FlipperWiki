# The draw

**When every player is out of balls, a random two-digit number appears on
[the `MATCH` display](../things/match-display.md), and every player whose score ends in those two
digits is given one more ball** ([0091](../../raw/rules/0091-when-every-player-is-out-of-balls-the.md)), played in player order like any other turn ([0091](../../raw/rules/0091-when-every-player-is-out-of-balls-the.md)).

**A score of nothing never wins** ([0091](../../raw/rules/0091-when-every-player-is-out-of-balls-the.md)).

**The width is the operator's** — one digit is a draw in ten, two the classic draw in a hundred
([0091](../../raw/rules/0091-when-every-player-is-out-of-balls-the.md)).

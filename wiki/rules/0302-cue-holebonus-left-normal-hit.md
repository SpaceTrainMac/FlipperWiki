# Rule 0302 — `HOLEBONUS_LEFT_NORMAL_HIT`

**At the glass: a rung lit on the left, and nothing paid** — the five rungs are worth 5 000 each
and **paid at redemption or at the end of the ball, never on the hit** ([0302](../../raw/rules/0302-cue-holebonus-left-normal-hit.md)). Pages: [what the machine says](../concepts/the-sounds.md), [the two hole-bonus ladders](../things/hole-bonus-ladders.md).

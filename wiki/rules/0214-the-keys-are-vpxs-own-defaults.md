# Rule 0214 — The keys are VPX's own defaults

**At the glass: Shift and Shift are the flippers, Enter is the plunger, `1` or `S` starts, Z, `/`
and Space nudge, and Esc leaves** — none of them rebound by this table ([0214](../../raw/rules/0214-the-keys-are-vpxs-own-defaults.md)). Pages:
[the Visual Pinball table](../bodies/vpx.md).

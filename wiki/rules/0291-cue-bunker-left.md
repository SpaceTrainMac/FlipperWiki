# Rule 0291 — `BUNKER_LEFT`

**At the glass: the left saucer has the ball** ([0291](../../raw/rules/0291-cue-bunker-left.md)). The cue is the bunker taking it, whether
that hit also starts [multiball](../concepts/multiball.md) or redeems
[the left hole bonus](../concepts/the-hole-bonus.md) ([0291](../../raw/rules/0291-cue-bunker-left.md)). Pages: [what the machine says](../concepts/the-sounds.md), [the left bunker](../things/left-bunker.md).

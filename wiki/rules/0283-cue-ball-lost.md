# Rule 0283 — `BALL_LOST`

**At the glass: the sound of losing a ball, and not necessarily of losing the turn** ([0283](../../raw/rules/0283-cue-ball-lost.md)). A
drain *inside* [the protection window](../concepts/drain-protection.md) is a different event with a
different cue, and in multiball this one fires while the turn carries on ([0283](../../raw/rules/0283-cue-ball-lost.md)). Pages: [what the machine says](../concepts/the-sounds.md), [drain protection](../concepts/drain-protection.md).

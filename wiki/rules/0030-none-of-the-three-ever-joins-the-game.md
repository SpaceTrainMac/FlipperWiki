# Rule 0030 — None of the three ever joins the game

**At the glass: hit a captive ball hard.** It is not in play and never will be; the score comes from
driving it up its ramp into the contact at the top, so a soft touch does nothing ([0030](../../raw/rules/0030-none-of-the-three-ever-joins-the-game.md)). Pages:
[the two centre captive balls](../things/centre-captive-balls.md),
[the right captive ball](../things/right-captive-ball.md).

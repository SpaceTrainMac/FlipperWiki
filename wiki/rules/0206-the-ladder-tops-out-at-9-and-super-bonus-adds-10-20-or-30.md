# Rule 0206 — Three independent sources agree

**At the glass: do not add the ladder and Super Bonus together — they are already one number.** The
ladder counts to 9 and Super Bonus supplies the tens, which is what makes the flyer's 39 come out;
three sources that do not depend on each other say so ([0206](../../raw/rules/0206-the-ladder-tops-out-at-9-and-super-bonus-adds-10-20-or-30.md)). Pages:
[the bonus ladder](../things/bonus-ladder.md).

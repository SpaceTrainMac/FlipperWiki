# Rule 0023 — The count is capped at 39, and the lamps are the number in two digits

**At the glass: read the bottom ladder as a number — the nine small lamps are the units and the
three Super Bonus lamps are the tens. Five lit and `10` and `20` means 25, which is 125 000 waiting**
([0023](../../raw/rules/0023-the-count-is-capped-at-39.md)). Pages: [the bonus ladder](../things/bonus-ladder.md), [the bonus count](../concepts/the-bonus-count.md).

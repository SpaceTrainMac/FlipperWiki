[machine.md § 1.5 The instruction cards - Pricing card - verbatim](../../../FlipperArchitecture/docs/machine.md#pricing-card--verbatim)

Prices are in **francs**, not pesetas, so this cabinet was on location in a French-speaking market —
provenance, not a rule, but a hint that route-specific settings may not be MAC's defaults. The three
"INDICATED ON DISPLAY" lines do matter: extra ball and both free plays are signalled on the
**backbox display**, not by a playfield lamp ([hardware.md §3](../../../FlipperArchitecture/docs/hardware.md#3-display-indicator-leds)).

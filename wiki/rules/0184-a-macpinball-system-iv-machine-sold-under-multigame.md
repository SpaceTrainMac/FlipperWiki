# Rule 0184 — A MacPinBall System IV machine, sold under MULTIGAME

**At the glass: this explains the machine's loose ends.** MAC's *MULTIGAME* scheme reused one
System IV chassis for every title and changed only the playfield, the backglass and the game
EPROM, so the switch and lamp matrices belong to the chassis rather than to Space Train — and a
switch or a lamp that does nothing in this game is the scheme working as intended, not a fault
([0184](../../raw/rules/0184-a-macpinball-system-iv-machine-sold-under-multigame.md)). Pages: [the 1987 machine](../bodies/1987.md), [every switch](../things/every-switch.md),
[the 48 playfield lamps](../things/playfield-lamps.md).

# A ball, a turn and a game

**One to four players, three balls each, taken in turn** ([0076](../../raw/rules/0076-one-to-four-players-three-balls-each-taken.md)).

| | |
|---|---|
| **a turn** | one ball served, played until no ball is in play and none is owed |
| **a ball** | from the plunger lane to the trough — several fit in one turn |
| **a game** | three turns a player, plus whatever the draw awards |

*(the table is rule 0076's, rendered for a player)*

**So a turn is not a ball**, and that is the distinction the rest of the machine is built on: an
extra ball and a saved ball both happen inside one turn.

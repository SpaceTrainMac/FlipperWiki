**Spelling it.** Each of the `M`, `A` and `C` lanes **toggles** its own letter: a lane whose lamp is
dark lights it, and a lane whose lamp is lit puts it out. The left and right flipper buttons rotate
all three letters one position — the lane-change idiom, and the reason a player who has `M` and `A`
lit and rolls the `C` lane by accident is not simply punished for it.

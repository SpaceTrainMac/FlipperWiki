[gameplay.md § 12. The ball, the turn and the game](../../../FlipperArchitecture/docs/gameplay.md#12-the-ball-the-turn-and-the-game)

**The ball it gives back does not count, and nothing is cleared for it.** No count runs, no lamp
goes out, the bonus and both hole ladders stand, and the player picks the same ball up where they
lost it.

**Upper playfield (FOTO04).** M-A-C rollover lanes across the top. Below the bumpers, three lamps
printed `30.000` — `EXTRA BALL` — `30.000`. **This section called them a three-target bank until
2026-09-05 and there are no targets there at all**: `D43` settled on 2026-09-01 that `M`, `A` and
`C` are the only contacts on the upper playfield and that those three lamps are the *display* of
M-A-C's three awards, lit through the arrows painted from each channel — see
[20260829-gameplay-mechanics.md](../../../FlipperArchitecture/docs/concepts/done/20260829-gameplay-mechanics.md). The reading was
made off a flyer and a photograph and it was wrong; a table built from it carries three drop targets
this machine does not have. Pop bumpers with blue caps: the matrix carries three (`BUMPER_LEFT`,
`_RIGHT`, `_MIDDLE`), two are clearly visible; position of the third **TBC**, and it is `Q3` in
[topography.json](../../../FlipperArchitecture/docs/topography.json). A numbered lane down the left edge, under the raised tunnel that runs down that edge, with its
own 5-step bonus ladder and `TRIPLE` / `DOUBLE`.

# The end of a ball

**When a ball ends, the playfield's lit awards are cleared and the M-A-C letters are not.** The
upper `EXTRA BALL` window goes with the rest of the playfield; `M`, `A` and `C` carry to the next
ball, and three lit letters start it complete and paying ([0010](../../raw/rules/0010-the-letters-carry-from-ball-to-ball-for.md)). TBC — what else the end of a ball
clears, counts and pays is not yet a rule here. See [M-A-C](../concepts/m-a-c.md).

[sound.md § 4. The catalogue - Songs, a playlist](../../../FlipperEngine/docs/sound.md#songs--songs-a-playlist)

**There is nothing to say about an entry beyond its path.** A playlist is a collection somebody put
in a folder; unlike a cue, no entry means anything to the engine and none of them has an event to be
described against. That is the whole reason [§5](../../../FlipperEngine/docs/sound.md#5-naming-what-a-cue-is-for-versus-what-it-is)
took the names away.

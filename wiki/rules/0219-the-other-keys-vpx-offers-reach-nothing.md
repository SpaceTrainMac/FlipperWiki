# Rule 0219 — The other keys VPX offers reach nothing

**At the glass: VPX's standard keyboard has controls this machine has no wiring for** ([0219](../../raw/rules/0219-the-other-keys-vpx-offers-reach-nothing.md)).
There is no coin switch in the matrix, so nothing the credit keys do reaches the game; and the tilt
key belongs to VPX rather than to the machine ([0219](../../raw/rules/0219-the-other-keys-vpx-offers-reach-nothing.md)). Pages:
[the Visual Pinball table](../bodies/vpx.md), [tilt](../concepts/tilt.md).

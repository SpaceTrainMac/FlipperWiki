**The backlight goes to white, not to black.** Nothing but `Intro` drives these three channels, so
whatever is left here stands until attract mode comes round again: a colour frozen wherever the sine
happened to stop is an accident on the backglass, and dark is a cabinet somebody has to go and check
on. White is neither.

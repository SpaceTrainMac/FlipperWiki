# Rule 0287 — `NO_MORE_EXTRABALL`

**At the glass: that was your last ball of the turn** ([0287](../../raw/rules/0287-cue-no-more-extraball.md)). Every extra ball adds one to the
two-digit field and every drain takes one away, so this is the drain that finds it empty ([0287](../../raw/rules/0287-cue-no-more-extraball.md)). Pages: [what the machine says](../concepts/the-sounds.md), [the status row](../things/status-row.md).

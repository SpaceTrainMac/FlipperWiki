[gameplay.md § 11. The end of a turn: the transfer](../../../FlipperArchitecture/docs/gameplay.md#11-the-end-of-a-turn-the-transfer)

**The whole end-of-turn ceremony is between 2.0 s and 6.8 s** — the count of
[§10](../../../FlipperArchitecture/docs/gameplay.md#10-the-end-of-a-ball-the-count) and then the transfer. Twelve turns of a four-player game at
the quiet figure is `12 × (1.6 + 2.0)` ≈ **43 seconds** of ceremony in one game.

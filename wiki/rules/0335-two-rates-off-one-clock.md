# Rule 0335 — Two rates off one clock

**At the glass: the colour changes twice as often as the line moves**, and both come off the same
50 ms beat ([0335](../../raw/rules/0335-two-rates-off-one-clock.md)). Pages: [attract mode](../moments/attract-mode.md), [the cabinet backlight](../things/cabinet-backlight.md).

# Rule 0161 — an armed 30 000 with no window running

****30 000** × that side's multiplier**, paid that side's bunker, or the end of the ball ([0161](../../raw/rules/0161-an-armed-30-000-with-no.md)). It is one row of
[every award, in one table](../concepts/every-award.md).

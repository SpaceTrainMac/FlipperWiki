**`MATCH` keeps the turn score, and that is the point.** Space Train Bonus is achieved with the ball
live ([§7](../../../FlipperArchitecture/docs/gameplay.md#the-two-the-flyer-names)), so the player is still scoring while the backglass above them
announces it. Taking the one moving number away to make room for the words would be the
announcement punishing the achievement.

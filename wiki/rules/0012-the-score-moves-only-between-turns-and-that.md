# Rule 0012 — The score moves only between turns

**At the glass: your own display will not move while you are playing, and that is not a fault.**
Watch the `MATCH` display instead — everything you earn lands there, and it goes onto your score when
the turn ends ([0012](../../raw/rules/0012-the-score-moves-only-between-turns-and-that.md)). Pages:
[what the machine keeps track of](../concepts/what-the-machine-tracks.md),
[the four player displays](../things/player-displays.md), [the `MATCH` display](../things/match-display.md),
[the end of a turn](../moments/end-of-turn.md).

[gameplay.md § 6. The hole bonuses, and the captive balls](../../../FlipperArchitecture/docs/gameplay.md#6-the-hole-bonuses-and-the-captive-balls)

**None of the three ever joins the game.** Each sits in a short guiding ramp closed to the
playfield, and the ball in play scores it by striking its underside hard enough to drive it up that
ramp into the contact at its top. So a hit here is a shot with force behind it rather than a touch,
the captive balls cost the two-ball store nothing ([§12](../../../FlipperArchitecture/docs/gameplay.md#12-the-ball-the-turn-and-the-game)), and
the eighteen twin hits at the foot of this section are eighteen shots aimed and landed.

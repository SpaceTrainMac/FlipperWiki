# Rule 0159 — one hole-bonus rung, lit at the end of the ball

****5 000** × that side's multiplier**, paid end of the ball ([0159](../../raw/rules/0159-one-hole-bonus-rung-lit-at-the.md)). It is one row of
[every award, in one table](../concepts/every-award.md).

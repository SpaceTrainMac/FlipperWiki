# Rule 0118 — `BUNKER_LEFT`

**Pays:** 100. **Advances:** starts **multiball** — a second ball is served — if `BALLSENSOR_BALLEJECTOR` has one ready, otherwise the ball is just spat back out. **Collects:** the left ladder's five rungs and marker, if that side's 30 s window is open; a left marker armed with no window running, at any time. ([0118](../../raw/rules/0118-bunker_left.md))

It is explained on [left bunker](../things/left-bunker.md).

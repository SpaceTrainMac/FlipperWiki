**The other keys VPX offers reach nothing.** There is no coin switch on this machine's matrix, so
the credit keys close no switch; the tilt key is VPX's own, and
[§5](../../../FlipperVPX/docs/using-the-table.md#5--what-it-does-that-a-cabinet-would-not) says what that means.

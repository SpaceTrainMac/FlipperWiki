# Rule 0256 — Seventeen entries in one ring

**At the glass: the menu is a ring rather than a list, so `EXIT` is one press back from `VOLUME`
instead of sixteen forward** ([0256](../../raw/rules/0256-seventeen-entries-in-one-ring.md)). Four entries change a sound, three change a duration or a
station, one resets the high scores on a three-second hold, two only report, and six leave the menu
([0256](../../raw/rules/0256-seventeen-entries-in-one-ring.md)). Pages: [the service menu](../moments/the-service-menu.md).

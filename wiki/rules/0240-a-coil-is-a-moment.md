# Rule 0240 — A coil is a moment

**At the glass: a coil flares and fades over two seconds, so three bumpers at different stages of
fade tell you which one fired first** ([0240](../../raw/rules/0240-a-coil-is-a-moment.md)). That is unreadable on a real playfield, and it is
often exactly the question being asked ([0240](../../raw/rules/0240-a-coil-is-a-moment.md)). Pages:
[the browser simulator](../bodies/simulator.md), [the thunderstrike](../concepts/the-thunderstrike.md).

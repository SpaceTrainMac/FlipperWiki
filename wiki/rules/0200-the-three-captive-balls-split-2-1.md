# Rule 0200 — The three captive balls split 2 + 1

**At the glass: when the apron says *center balls* it means the two twins, and *right ball* means
the one above them on the right** ([0200](../../raw/rules/0200-the-three-captive-balls-split-2-1.md)). The card's split is the switch matrix's split —
`BALLWITHBALL_LEFT_TWIN` and `_RIGHT_TWIN` against `BALLWITHBALL_RIGHT_TOP` — so the 1987 wording
translates without any guesswork ([0200](../../raw/rules/0200-the-three-captive-balls-split-2-1.md)). Pages:
[the two centre captive balls](../things/centre-captive-balls.md),
[the right captive ball](../things/right-captive-ball.md),
[the two cards on the apron](../things/apron-cards.md).

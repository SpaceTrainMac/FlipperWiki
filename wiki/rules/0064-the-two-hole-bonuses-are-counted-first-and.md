# Rule 0064 — Hole bonuses first, the ladder last

**At the glass: the order is the card's — both hole sides, then the ladder. The `DOUBLE` and
`TRIPLE` lamps never go out** ([0064](../../raw/rules/0064-the-two-hole-bonuses-are-counted-first-and.md)). Pages: [the end of a ball](../moments/end-of-ball.md).

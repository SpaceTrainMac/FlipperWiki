# Rule 0160 — a side's 5th hit, then its own bunker or the same twin inside 30 s

**that side's five rungs + **30 000**, × its multiplier**, paid at once (windowed) ([0160](../../raw/rules/0160-a-sides-5th-hit-then-its.md)). It is one row of
[every award, in one table](../concepts/every-award.md).

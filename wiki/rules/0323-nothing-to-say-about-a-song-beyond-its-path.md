# Rule 0323 — There is nothing to say about an entry beyond its path

**At the glass: no song on this machine is attached to anything** — unlike a cue, an entry in the
playlist means nothing to the machine and there is nothing to describe it against ([0323](../../raw/rules/0323-nothing-to-say-about-a-song-beyond-its-path.md)). Pages:
[what the machine says](../concepts/the-sounds.md).

# Rule 0060 — What the right bunker does

**At the glass: 100, an extra ball if either hole ladder is past its second rung, and the right
redemption if it is blinking — then it kicks the ball out after five seconds** ([0060](../../raw/rules/0060-bunker_right-is-the-cards-hole-for-the-right.md)). Pages:
[the right bunker](../things/right-bunker.md), [the extra ball](../concepts/extra-ball.md).

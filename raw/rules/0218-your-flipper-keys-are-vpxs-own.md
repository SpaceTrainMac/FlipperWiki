**Your flipper keys are VPX's own, and the engine can only switch them off.** On the real cabinet the
buttons drive the flipper coils directly in hardware and the engine's only say is a relay that gates
the whole circuit; the table does exactly that, so the ball, the flipper and the key are all inside
VPX's own loop. Nothing about this bridge's speed can make the machine feel bad to play — and when the
game does not want you flipping, the flippers go dead rather than sluggish.

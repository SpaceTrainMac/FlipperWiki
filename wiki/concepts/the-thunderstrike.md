# The thunderstrike

**All three bumpers inside one second pays a flat 500, on top of the 300 they have already paid**
([0054](../../raw/rules/0054-landing-all-three-bumpers-within-one-second-of.md)). [The backlight](../things/cabinet-backlight.md) flashes white four times — two short at
300 ms, two long at 600 ms, about 300 ms apart ([0054](../../raw/rules/0054-landing-all-three-bumpers-within-one-second-of.md)).

**It rewards a hurry, not a skill shot.** Nothing multiplies it, nothing caps how often it repeats
in a ball, and the order of the three does not matter — only that all three closed inside the one
second ([0054](../../raw/rules/0054-landing-all-three-bumpers-within-one-second-of.md)).

**What moves is a horizontal line, and it crosses the whole cabinet.** The rise starts at the house
ball at the bottom of the playfield, climbs it row by row, carries on up into the backglass and ends
at the M-A-C lanes' equivalent up there — the player 1 and 2 score displays at the top of the glass.
Then it comes back down the same way.

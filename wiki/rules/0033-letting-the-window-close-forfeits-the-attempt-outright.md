# Rule 0033 — A closed window forfeits the attempt; a drain does not

**At the glass: run the 30 seconds out and that side pays nothing at all. Lose the ball with the
lamps still blinking and it pays what is lit** — so the clock is a risk the live ball carries, not a
reason to fear a drain ([0033](../../raw/rules/0033-letting-the-window-close-forfeits-the-attempt-outright.md)). Pages: [the hole bonus](../concepts/the-hole-bonus.md),
[the two hole-bonus ladders](../things/hole-bonus-ladders.md), [the end of a ball](../moments/end-of-ball.md).

**The card's `x BONUS` is the ladder, and it was never a multiplier.** The lower ladder is printed
`BONUS X 5000` vertically down its side ([machine.md §1.4](../../../FlipperArchitecture/docs/machine.md#14-playfield-geography)), so
*lights x BONUS* is a lamp on that ladder lighting — `BON1_5K_BOT` on the first completion and the
next one on every completion after.

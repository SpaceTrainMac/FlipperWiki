# Rule 0339 — Neither gesture can be mistaken for the other

**At the glass: a hand landing late does not start the wrong thing, and a hand slipping off does
not turn a service hold into a radio hold** ([0339](../../raw/rules/0339-neither-gesture-can-be-mistaken-for-the-other.md)). Pages: [attract mode](../moments/attract-mode.md).

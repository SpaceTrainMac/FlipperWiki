# Rule 0050 — Collecting SPECIAL costs the ball

**At the glass: the shot that pays 5 000 is the shot that drains — so a lit `SPECIAL` changes how
you play the bottom of the machine. The exception is `HOUSE BALL` flashing: then it is free**
([0050](../../raw/rules/0050-collecting-special-costs-the-ball.md)). Pages: [the special](../concepts/special.md), [the outlanes](../things/outlanes.md),
[the `HOUSE BALL` lamp](../things/house-ball-lamp.md).

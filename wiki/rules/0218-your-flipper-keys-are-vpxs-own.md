# Rule 0218 — Your flipper keys are VPX's own

**At the glass: the flippers cannot feel laggy, because nothing between the key and the flipper is
the game** ([0218](../../raw/rules/0218-your-flipper-keys-are-vpxs-own.md)). On the cabinet the buttons drive the coils in hardware and the software's only
power is a relay that cuts the circuit; the table copies that, so when the game takes the flippers
away they go dead rather than slow ([0218](../../raw/rules/0218-your-flipper-keys-are-vpxs-own.md)). Pages:
[the flipper buttons](../things/flipper-buttons.md), [the Visual Pinball table](../bodies/vpx.md).

# The SPECIAL lamps

**`SPECIAL` on both outlanes lights the moment M-A-C is completed** — all three letters lit at
once ([0005](../../raw/rules/0005-completing-it.md)) — and lights again on the next ball if the three letters were still lit when the
last one drained, because the letters carry and the completion re-runs ([0010](../../raw/rules/0010-the-letters-carry-from-ball-to-ball-for.md)). **The third
completion of [the `ADVANCE BONUS` bank](../concepts/advance-bonus-bank.md) lights them too** ([0016](../../raw/rules/0016-the-fourth-target-completes-the-bank.md)). TBC — what a lit `SPECIAL` pays, and how it is collected, is
not yet a rule here. The mechanic that lights it is [M-A-C](../concepts/m-a-c.md); the award is
[the special](../concepts/special.md).

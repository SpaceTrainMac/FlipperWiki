[cabinet.md § 3. Sound sets - Where the sets live, and which one plays](../../../FlipperEngine/docs/cabinet.md#where-the-sets-live-and-which-one-plays)

**`ALL SETS` pools the five into one cabinet.** Every cue then draws from every set that maps it, and
the playlists are concatenated — so a bumper may answer in chiptune, in an orchestra, in a voice or
from a band, and the jukebox plays all forty pieces. It is one setting and not a mode: `SFX MODE` still
decides how a cue with several files picks between them, and now that pool is five sets deep. **There
is one mixer and no ducking**, so the sets are heard at whatever levels their authors chose; five
sets that were each mastered sensibly are not a set that was mastered together
([concept 20260827](../../../FlipperEngine/docs/concepts/done/20260827-sfx-variants.md)).

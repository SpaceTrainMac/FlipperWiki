# Rule 0223 — `./run_vpx.sh -B` leaves the region exactly as it is

**At the glass: the drawn backbox carries all of it** — four staggered player scores, the status
row bottom left, `MATCH` bottom right, the sixteen indicator plates, and the RGB backlight as three
round lamps that fade ([0223](../../raw/rules/0223-run-vpx-b-leaves-the-region-as-it-is.md)). Anybody who has placed the region by hand keeps it by launching
with `-B` ([0223](../../raw/rules/0223-run-vpx-b-leaves-the-region-as-it-is.md)). Pages: [the Visual Pinball table](../bodies/vpx.md),
[the cabinet backlight](../things/cabinet-backlight.md).

[gameplay.md § 7. The three multipliers](../../../FlipperArchitecture/docs/gameplay.md#7-the-three-multipliers)

**All three survive the ball and the game.** A player who reaches ×2 on the bottom pair on ball 1
counts their bonus at ×2 for the rest of the game, and the same is true of each hole ladder. The
lamps stay lit across the turn and are not walked out when a collection spends the count they
multiplied.

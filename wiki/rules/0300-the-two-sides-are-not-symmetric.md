# Rule 0300 — Each side is a five-hit build and then a timed redemption

**At the glass: a redemption is six things being paid at once, on either side** — five rungs and
the marker ([0300](../../raw/rules/0300-the-two-sides-are-not-symmetric.md)). The two sides were not symmetric in sound until 2026-09-04, when the left was
given the six lettered cues the right already had ([0300](../../raw/rules/0300-the-two-sides-are-not-symmetric.md)). Pages:
[what the machine says](../concepts/the-sounds.md), [the hole bonus](../concepts/the-hole-bonus.md).

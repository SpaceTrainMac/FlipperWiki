**Three independent pairs of `DOUBLE` / `TRIPLE` lamps, and each multiplies its own collection and
nothing else.**

| Multiplier | Lamps | Earned by | Multiplies |
|---|---|---|---|
| **bottom** | `X2_BOT`, `X3_BOT` | the 40th and 50th total bank completion of the advance bonus, collected at the right captive ball | the bonus count — 5 000 a bonus, up to 39 of them |
| **left hole** | `BON_X2_TOP_L`, `BON_X3_TOP_L` | the 2nd and 3rd successful left-ladder redemption | the left ladder's rungs and its armed marker |
| **right hole** | `BON_X2_5K_R`, `BON_X3_5K_R` | the 2nd and 3rd successful right-ladder redemption | the right ladder's rungs and its armed marker |

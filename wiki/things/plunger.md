# The plunger

**Every ball is fired by hand.** A served ball waits in the start ramp and the spring plunger shoots
it onto the playfield; there is no auto-launch on this cabinet ([0078](../../raw/rules/0078-a-ball-begins-in-the-start-ramp-and.md)). The switch at the end of the
ramp, [`OUTFIRE`](outfire.md), is what sees it go ([0078](../../raw/rules/0078-a-ball-begins-in-the-start-ramp-and.md)).

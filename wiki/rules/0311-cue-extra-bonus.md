# Rule 0311 — `EXTRA_BONUS`

**At the glass: the largest single award on the machine, and the rarest** ([0311](../../raw/rules/0311-cue-extra-bonus.md)). Once or twice
in a whole game — the 40th and 50th bank completion — against a ball that collects a few thousand
at a time ([0311](../../raw/rules/0311-cue-extra-bonus.md)). Pages: [what the machine says](../concepts/the-sounds.md), [the extra bonus](../concepts/the-extra-bonus.md).

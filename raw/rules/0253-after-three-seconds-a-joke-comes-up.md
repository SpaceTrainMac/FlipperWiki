**After three seconds the light show stops and a joke comes up on the score displays.** That is the
cabinet telling you it is counting — keep holding for the remaining six and the menu opens; let go and
attract mode carries on where it left off. If you are ever unsure whether the gesture is working, that
is the thing to wait for.

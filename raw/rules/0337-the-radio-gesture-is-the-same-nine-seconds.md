[intro.md § IntroMachine — The two gestures](../../../FlipperEngine/docs/states/intro.md#the-two-gestures)

The radio gesture is the same nine seconds as the service one on purpose: two holds on the same two
buttons that ran to different lengths would be two things to learn, and one of them would be got
wrong every time. It exists because the alternative was waiting — the radio arrives by itself after
`radioIdleSeconds`, which is right for a cabinet nobody is standing at and useless to somebody who is
standing at it and wants the radio on now.

**`Space Ship Bonus` and `Space Train Bonus` are these multipliers reaching ×3, announced.** They
are not a tenth mechanic: they are what the machine does when the three pairs above finish.

| | Achieved when | The machine |
|---|---|---|
| **Space Ship Bonus** | `X3_BOT` lights — the 50th bank completion, collected | plays a *space-engine warp* |
| **Space Train Bonus** | `X3_BOT`, `BON_X3_TOP_L` and `BON_X3_5K_R` are **all** lit | starts a steam train and flies it away, reads **`SPACE TRAIN LIFTS OFF`** across the four player displays for five seconds — one word each ([§13](../../../FlipperArchitecture/docs/gameplay.md#the-space-train-banner)) — and fires the knocker **three times** |

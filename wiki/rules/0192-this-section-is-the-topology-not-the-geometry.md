# Rule 0192 — This section is the topology, not the geometry

**At the glass: the flyer says what connects to what and never where anything is** ([0192](../../raw/rules/0192-this-section-is-the-topology-not-the-geometry.md)). Every
position — the deck, the routes, the posts, the wire rails, the plastics — comes from the
photographs of the cabinet instead, and the table that plays today is measured against those
([0192](../../raw/rules/0192-this-section-is-the-topology-not-the-geometry.md)). Pages: [the two levels](../concepts/the-two-levels.md).

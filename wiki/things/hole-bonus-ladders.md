# The two hole-bonus ladders

**Five rungs and a `HOLE BONUS 30000` lamp on each side, and each side is collected at its own
bunker.** A side's `30 000` is lit by that ladder reaching its fifth hit, or by the right captive
ball; it is collected at that side's bunker, or at the end of the ball ([0018](../../raw/rules/0018-the-lit-awards-wait-to-be-collected-which.md)). Each ladder lasts
one ball ([0011](../../raw/rules/0011-ten-quantities-and-everything-below-is-one-of.md)), and a drain clears both ([0013](../../raw/rules/0013-six-things-carry-from-one-ball-to-the.md)).

**Four hits light four rungs; the fifth lights the last rung and the `30 000` lamp together, and
sets all six blinking** — the start of a configurable 30-second window ([0031](../../raw/rules/0031-each-side-is-a-five-hit-build-then-a.md)). A redemption is on
that timer, and six lamps blinking on one side is how it says so ([0019](../../raw/rules/0019-four-awards-are-lit-on-a-timer-instead.md), [0031](../../raw/rules/0031-each-side-is-a-five-hit-build-then-a.md)).

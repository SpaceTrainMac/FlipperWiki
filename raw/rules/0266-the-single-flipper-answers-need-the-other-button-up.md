[cabinet.md § 2. The service menu - The radio, and the three buttons in it](../../../FlipperEngine/docs/cabinet.md#the-radio-and-the-three-buttons-in-it)

**The single-flipper answers need the other button up.** Reaching for both flippers does not step a
station, does not move the volume and does not drop you out of the state, whichever hand lands
first.

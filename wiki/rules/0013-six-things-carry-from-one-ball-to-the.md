# Rule 0013 — Six things carry from one ball to the next

**At the glass: a drain does not take your multipliers, your letters or your extra balls won — it
takes the bonus count, both hole ladders and every lit award** ([0013](../../raw/rules/0013-six-things-carry-from-one-ball-to-the.md)). Pages:
[what the machine keeps track of](../concepts/what-the-machine-tracks.md),
[the end of a ball](../moments/end-of-ball.md), [the bonus ladder](../things/bonus-ladder.md).

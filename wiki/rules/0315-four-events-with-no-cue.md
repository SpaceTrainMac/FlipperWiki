# Rule 0315 — Four events with no cue

**At the glass: three of the four silences are deliberate and one is a gap the document admits to**
([0315](../../raw/rules/0315-four-events-with-no-cue.md)). The transfer already sounds like something, the 5th hole-bonus hit speaks like the four
before it, and the thunderstrike is announced in light — but **a hole-bonus window expiring
uncollected costs 55 000 and says nothing**, and no cue was invented for it ([0315](../../raw/rules/0315-four-events-with-no-cue.md)). Pages:
[what the machine says](../concepts/the-sounds.md), [the hole bonus](../concepts/the-hole-bonus.md).

# The X BONUS lamp

**`X_BON` flashing means a 20-second window on the extra bonus, and it opens at your 40th and 50th
completion of the `ADVANCE BONUS` bank** ([0019](../../raw/rules/0019-four-awards-are-lit-on-a-timer-instead.md)). Those are the only two times it opens, and the
count that reaches them is one of the things the machine tracks and shows nowhere else ([0011](../../raw/rules/0011-ten-quantities-and-everything-below-is-one-of.md)). **It
is a total across the game, not this ball's ladder** — it never resets, so the 40th completion can
land on any ball ([0026](../../raw/rules/0026-a-separate-hidden-count-tracks-bank-completions-across.md)).

# Rule 0186 — Source material

**At the glass: the apron photograph is the single most valuable thing anybody has of this
machine**, because it is the only witness to the two instruction cards; the two playfield
photographs and the flyer's front and diagram carry the rest, and the remaining seven scans show
backglass artwork and wiring and settle nothing about play ([0186](../../raw/rules/0186-source-material.md)). Pages:
[the 1987 machine](../bodies/1987.md).

**There is a second gesture on the same two buttons: the right flipper on its own, held for nine
seconds, starts the radio.** It reads the same way — at three seconds the light show stops and the
glass says `RADIO` over a line of scrolling nonsense — and the two cannot be confused, because a
single-flipper hold only counts while the other button stays up. If you reach for both and one hand
lands first, nothing happens; and if you let go of one hand halfway through the service gesture, that
does *not* turn the rest of it into a request for the radio. Let go of both and start again.

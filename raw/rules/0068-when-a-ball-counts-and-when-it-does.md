**When a ball counts, and when it does not:**

| The ball ended in | Counted |
|---|---|
| a drain, and the turn is over | **yes** |
| a drain, and an extra ball follows | **yes**, before the playfield is cleared for the extra ball |
| drain protection giving it back | **no** — the ball did not end, and nothing is cleared |
| the cabinet being switched off | **no** — nothing is counted out, and nothing is lost |
| a tilt | as a drain — see [§12](../../../FlipperArchitecture/docs/gameplay.md#12-the-ball-the-turn-and-the-game) |

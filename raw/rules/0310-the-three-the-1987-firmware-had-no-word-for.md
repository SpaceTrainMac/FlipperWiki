**These three are what the 1987 firmware had no word for.** Every cue above was transcribed from the
sound player this cabinet ran before the engine existed, and it stopped where the original machine's
behaviour stopped: `Space Ship Bonus` and `Space Train Bonus` were words on a flyer rather than
sounds, and the extra bonus is a rule this project decided.

[gameplay.md § 5. The bonus count](../../../FlipperArchitecture/docs/gameplay.md#5-the-bonus-count)

**Nothing about the count is paid as it climbs.** The 100 an advance-bonus target pays is the
switch's own award, paid whether or not that hit completes the bank; the 5 000 an inlane pays is
likewise unaffected. What is deferred is the bonus, which is a count until the ball is over.

# Rule 0232 — The playfield was flat, until 2026-09-06

**At the glass: this is the proof that the two levels are scenery.** For months the table had no
upper deck — the saucers kicked the ball up a flat playfield — and every rule of the machine
still worked, because none of the four routes between the storeys carries a switch ([0232](../../raw/rules/0232-the-playfield-was-flat-until-2026-09-06.md)). Since
2026-09-06 the saucers lift the ball onto a real deck ([0232](../../raw/rules/0232-the-playfield-was-flat-until-2026-09-06.md)). Pages:
[the two levels](../concepts/the-two-levels.md), [the Visual Pinball table](../bodies/vpx.md).

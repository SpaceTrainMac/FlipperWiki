# Rule 0018 — The lit awards wait to be collected

**At the glass: a lit lamp is not a payment, it is an errand.** `SPECIAL` waits at either outlane,
the right `EXTRA BALL` at the right captive ball, a `HOLE BONUS 30000` at its own bunker — and every
one of them is lost when the ball ends ([0018](../../raw/rules/0018-the-lit-awards-wait-to-be-collected-which.md)). Pages:
[lit awards](../concepts/lit-awards.md), [the outlanes](../things/outlanes.md),
[the two hole-bonus ladders](../things/hole-bonus-ladders.md), [the special](../concepts/special.md),
[the extra ball](../concepts/extra-ball.md), [the right captive ball](../things/right-captive-ball.md).

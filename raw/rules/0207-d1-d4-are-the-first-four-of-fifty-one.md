**D1–D4 are the first four of fifty-one, and the other forty-seven are not here.** These four were
taken on 2026-08-26 and settled values the sources leave blank. Everything decided since — D20–D50,
which answer what happens at every switch, what every lamp means and how a ball, a turn and a game
end — is in
[concepts/done/20260829-gameplay-mechanics.md](../../../FlipperArchitecture/docs/concepts/done/20260829-gameplay-mechanics.md), and
the behaviour they produce is [gameplay.md](../../../FlipperArchitecture/docs/gameplay.md). **A reader who stops at this section is
told the sources are silent about things that have since been answered**, which is why this
paragraph is here and why the four below are not copied anywhere else.

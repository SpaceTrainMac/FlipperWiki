# Rule 0238 — That last row is what makes the service menu reachable

**At the glass: hold `ArrowLeft` and `ArrowRight` together for nine seconds and the service menu
opens** ([0238](../../raw/rules/0238-cabinet-buttons-are-held-not-tapped.md)). It works only because the three cabinet buttons stay closed for as long as the
key is down; a button that released itself immediately could never reach a nine-second gesture
([0238](../../raw/rules/0238-cabinet-buttons-are-held-not-tapped.md)). Pages: [the browser simulator](../bodies/simulator.md).

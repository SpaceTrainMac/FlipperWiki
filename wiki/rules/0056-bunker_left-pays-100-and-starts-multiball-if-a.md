# Rule 0056 — The left bunker pays 100 and starts multiball

**At the glass: the left saucer is 100, and if the machine has a ball ready it starts multiball —
then waits for you to plunge the second ball, and lets the first one out 2.5 seconds later** ([0056](../../raw/rules/0056-bunker_left-pays-100-and-starts-multiball-if-a.md)).

**Collecting `SPECIAL` costs the ball.** Both `SPECIAL` switches are outlanes, so the shot that pays
5 000 is the shot that drains — which is the trade the original playfield printed, and the reason
`SPECIAL` being lit changes how a player plays the bottom of the machine rather than just adding to
the score. The one exception is drain protection: for the 15 seconds that window is open, a lit
`SPECIAL` pays and no longer costs the ball ([§12](../../../FlipperArchitecture/docs/gameplay.md#12-the-ball-the-turn-and-the-game)).

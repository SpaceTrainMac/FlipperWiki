[gameplay.md § 4. The advance-bonus bank, and the combination](../../../FlipperArchitecture/docs/gameplay.md#4-the-advance-bonus-bank-and-the-combination)

**The fourth target completes the bank**: all four lamps flash three times, then go out; the bank
pays **10 000**; the bonus count advances one step, lighting the next `BON#_5K_BOT` lamp or rolling
a Super Bonus decade exactly as [§5](../../../FlipperArchitecture/docs/gameplay.md#5-the-bonus-count) describes; and the *combination* advances
one stage. The bank can then be built again from empty, so three completions are twelve target hits
at the very least, and the 40th is a hundred and sixty.

| Completion | Awards |
|---|---|
| **1st** | one lamp on the bonus ladder — `BON1_5K_BOT` from empty |
| **2nd** | the next ladder lamp, and the right `EXTRA BALL` lights |
| **3rd** | the next ladder lamp, `SPECIAL` lights, and an **extra ball is awarded outright** |

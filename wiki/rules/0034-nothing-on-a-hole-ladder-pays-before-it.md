# Rule 0034 — Nothing on a hole ladder pays before it is redeemed

**At the glass: the rungs are promises. 5 000 each and 30 000 for the marker, at that side's
multiplier, and you are paid at the redemption or when the ball ends** ([0034](../../raw/rules/0034-nothing-on-a-hole-ladder-pays-before-it.md)). Pages:
[the hole bonus](../concepts/the-hole-bonus.md), [the two hole-bonus ladders](../things/hole-bonus-ladders.md),
[the end of a ball](../moments/end-of-ball.md).

# Multiball

**The left bunker starts it, and only if a ball is staged and ready to serve** ([0056](../../raw/rules/0056-bunker_left-pays-100-and-starts-multiball-if-a.md)).

**What happens is choreographed**: a second ball is served into the start ramp from the store, the
bunker holds your first ball while it is on its way, and releases it **2.5 seconds after the second
ball leaves the ramp** — so both are on the playfield together ([0056](../../raw/rules/0056-bunker_left-pays-100-and-starts-multiball-if-a.md)).

**The second ball waits for your plunger like any other.** This cabinet has no auto-launch, so the
hold lasts as long as you take over it, and the 2.5 seconds is measured from the plunge and not from
the serve ([0056](../../raw/rules/0056-bunker_left-pays-100-and-starts-multiball-if-a.md)).

**The machine has exactly two balls, not the flyer's three, and multiball cannot be fed a third.**
While both are on the playfield the store reads empty, so a left-bunker hit during multiball finds
no ball to serve: the whole choreography is skipped for that hit and the bunker just spits the ball
straight back out ([0057](../../raw/rules/0057-the-machine-has-exactly-two-balls-not-the.md)).

See [the left bunker](../things/left-bunker.md).

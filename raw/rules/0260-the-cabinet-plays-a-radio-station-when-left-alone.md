[cabinet.md § 2. The service menu - The radio, and the three buttons in it](../../../FlipperEngine/docs/cabinet.md#the-radio-and-the-three-buttons-in-it)

**The cabinet plays a radio station when it has been left alone**, after `RADIO AFTER`. It is a
state like any other, not a screensaver: a slower version of the same light show, the station's name
across the four score displays, and `RADIO` on `MATCH` — or `NO NET` while it is failing to connect.

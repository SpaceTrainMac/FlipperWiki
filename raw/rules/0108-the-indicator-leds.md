`ACTIVE PLR 1…4` — whose turn it is. `GAME OVER` — no game running. `HIGH SCORE` — the record
changed hands. `MATCH` — the draw hit. `TILT` — an indicator with no switch behind it, because tilt
never reaches the host. `CREDITS` — dark for the whole of a game, like the two-digit field it
labels, because this cabinet has none. `EXTRA BALL` left and right, and `EXTRA PLAY`, are the
backbox's own announcement of an award the card says is *indicated on display*. With the four `×10`
of [*past a million*](../../../FlipperArchitecture/docs/gameplay.md#past-a-million) above, that is all sixteen.

# Rule 0427 — Addresses 232 to 240

**At the glass: nine coils are the whole of what this machine can do to a ball** — the ejector,
two slingshots, two saucer kickers, three bumpers and the knocker ([0427](../../raw/rules/0427-the-nine-coils.md)). **Each de-energises
automatically**, so nothing can hold one on ([0427](../../raw/rules/0427-the-nine-coils.md)). Pages: [the nine coils](../things/the-coils.md).

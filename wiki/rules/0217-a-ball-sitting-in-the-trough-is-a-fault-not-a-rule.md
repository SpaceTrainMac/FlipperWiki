# Rule 0217 — If a ball ever does sit in the trough and stay there, that is a fault

**At the glass: nothing you can press will fetch it, and you are not missing a step** ([0217](../../raw/rules/0217-a-ball-sitting-in-the-trough-is-a-fault-not-a-rule.md)).
Until 2026-09-05 the machine really did wait — thirty seconds, for a press nobody knew to make,
announced by one lamp among forty-eight — and that is fixed and held by a test ([0217](../../raw/rules/0217-a-ball-sitting-in-the-trough-is-a-fault-not-a-rule.md)). Pages:

# Rule 0058 — The left bunker collects as well as starting multiball

**At the glass: one shot at the left saucer can pay the hole bonus and start multiball at the same
time — and if a marker is sitting armed with no clock on it, this is where you collect it, whenever
you like** ([0058](../../raw/rules/0058-it-is-also-the-left-hole-ladders-redemption.md)). Pages: [the left bunker](../things/left-bunker.md),
[the hole bonus](../concepts/the-hole-bonus.md).

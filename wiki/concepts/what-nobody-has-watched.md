# What nobody has watched

**Nobody has seen a single one of these mechanics run on the cabinet.** Every duration on these
pages is arithmetic rather than evidence ([0179](../../raw/rules/0179-nobody-has-seen-a-single-one-of-these.md)).

**Two of them are the first things a person standing at the machine will want to change**: the
43 seconds a quiet four-player game spends [counting](../moments/end-of-turn.md), and
[the knocker](../things/knocker.md) landing in the middle of a two-second count-up ([0179](../../raw/rules/0179-nobody-has-seen-a-single-one-of-these.md)).

**Real machines speed the bonus count up as it runs, and this one does not.** One pace is enough
until somebody has watched [the 4.8-second case](../moments/end-of-ball.md); a pace that changes is
much harder to reason about than one that does not ([0180](../../raw/rules/0180-real-machines-speed-the-bonus-count-up-as.md)).

**This is the page an evening at the glass is for.** Write what you saw into `raw/observations/`,
dated and naming the body, and the ingest will put it against the rules it touches — see
[the README](../../README.md).

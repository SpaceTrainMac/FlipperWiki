**Reaching ×3 on one side takes at least eighteen hits on that twin, none of them at a bunker.**
Three redemptions light `DOUBLE` on the 2nd and `TRIPLE` on the 3rd, and the cheapest way to redeem
is the twin itself: five hits to build, a sixth inside the window to redeem, six per cycle, eighteen
for three. A player who redeems at the bunker instead pays the same five hits plus a shot somewhere
else on the playfield — no cheaper on the twin, only slower.

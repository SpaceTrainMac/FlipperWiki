# The advance-bonus bank, and the combination

**Four `ADVANCE BONUS` targets on the lower playfield, and the only three-stage award on the
machine** ([0014](../../raw/rules/0014-four-advance-bonus-targets-on-the-lower-playfield.md)). Complete the bank three times and each completion is worth more than the last.

**A hit is 100 and a lamp, and nothing else.** The first hit on each target pays 100 and lights its
lamp; a second hit on a lit target does nothing, and two struck in the same instant both count.
**Hitting a target does not move the bonus count** — that waits for the bank to complete ([0015](../../raw/rules/0015-each-target-on-its-first-hit-pays-100.md)).

**The fourth target completes the bank.** All four lamps flash three times and go out, the bank pays
**10 000**, the bonus count advances one step, and the combination advances one stage. The bank can
then be built again from empty ([0016](../../raw/rules/0016-the-fourth-target-completes-the-bank.md)).

| Completion | What it lights |
|---|---|
| **1st** | one lamp on [the bonus ladder](../things/bonus-ladder.md) — the first rung, from empty |
| **2nd** | the next rung, and [the right `EXTRA BALL`](../things/extra-ball-right.md) lights |
| **3rd** | the next rung, [`SPECIAL`](special.md) lights, and **an extra ball is awarded outright** |

**A completion is worth one bonus and a `5000` inlane is worth five** ([0022](../../raw/rules/0022-what-advances-it.md)), so the bank is the
slow way to fill [the ladder](../things/bonus-ladder.md) and the reliable one.

**A completion's *`x BONUS`* is a lamp, not a multiplier.** The ladder is printed `BONUS X 5000`
down its side, and what a completion lights is the next rung ([0017](../../raw/rules/0017-the-cards-x-bonus-is-the-ladder-and.md)).

**So the combination is expensive.** Three completions are twelve target hits at the very least, and
the 40th completion is a hundred and sixty ([0016](../../raw/rules/0016-the-fourth-target-completes-the-bank.md)).
See [the four `ADVANCE BONUS` targets](../things/advance-bonus-targets.md).

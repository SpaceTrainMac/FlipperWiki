[cabinet.md § 3. Sound sets - The five sets, and what each one turns the cabinet into](../../../FlipperEngine/docs/cabinet.md#the-five-sets-and-what-each-one-turns-the-cabinet-into)

**There are five of them in [FlipperSounds](../../../FlipperSounds), and they are five different
machines.** Every set answers the same forty effects, the same three music cues and one
playlist; what changes is who is answering — and, since 2026-09-04, **how far each set has got**:
eleven of the forty were added that day and a set that has not caught up is quieter rather than
broken, because a cue with no entry is silent ([sound.md §4](../../../FlipperEngine/docs/sound.md#4-the-catalogue)). Each set's own `README.md` is it explaining itself at
length, and the icons here are the sets' own.

| | Set | What the cabinet becomes |
|---|---|---|
| ![The 8-bit set's icon](../../../FlipperSounds/8-bit/icon.svg) | **`8-bit`** — *Insert coin* | An arcade in 1987. Two pulse channels, a triangle and a noise register in the dialect of an NES, a Game & Watch and a PC speaker, with every bumper hit sounding like a new high score. **Generated from scores by a software 2A03** — nothing in it is a recording of anything |
| ![The rool set's icon](../../../FlipperSounds/rool/icon.svg) | **`rool`** — *The coolest sound set ever* | Pop culture at full volume: lightsabers igniting, droids chirping, heroes arriving. Thirteen songs and a bumper that opens a blockbuster. **RooL's own set, assembled by hand out of records and films**, and the one this cabinet grew up with |
| ![The space-opera set's icon](../../../FlipperSounds/space-opera/icon.svg) | **`space-opera`** — *When the galaxy was larger* | A command deck. Full orchestra, lush and wide, panned across the cabinet — the opposite of `8-bit` at every point. **Composed as notation and rendered through FluidSynth**, and likewise a recording of nothing |
| ![The vocals set's icon](../../../FlipperSounds/vocals/icon.svg) | **`vocals`** — *Narrated adventures of Mac and Miss Marlboro* | A cabinet that talks to you. Almost nothing is music: the bumpers announce themselves, the ball sighs as it drains, and the jukebox tells nine stories instead of playing nine songs. **Spoken by Piper TTS from text scripts**, in several character voices |
| ![The rock-opera set's icon](../../../FlipperSounds/rock-opera/icon.svg) | **`rock-opera`** — *The Wizard will see you now* | A band in the room, dry and loud and close. Overdriven guitar, Hammond organ, a bass in eighths and a big dry kit — and **a voice that sings rather than speaks**: Mac sings the verses, the Lady on the Glass the one slow number, a Chorus the refrains. Every effect is an instrument of that band, down to the palm-muted chop for the bumper. **Notation through FluidSynth with a DiffSinger voicebank in front of it** — and the one set a fresh clone cannot build unattended, because the bank is a download a person has to accept the terms of |

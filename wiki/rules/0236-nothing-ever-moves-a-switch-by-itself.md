# Rule 0236 — Nothing in the simulator ever moves a switch by itself

**At the glass: this is the one thing that makes the simulator behave unlike a machine** — a coil
firing does not open the switch a ball would have been sitting on, because there is no ball
([0236](../../raw/rules/0236-nothing-ever-moves-a-switch-by-itself.md)). Every strange state on this body starts here: a bunker that will not let go, or a serve
after every single drain ([0236](../../raw/rules/0236-nothing-ever-moves-a-switch-by-itself.md)). Pages: [the browser simulator](../bodies/simulator.md).

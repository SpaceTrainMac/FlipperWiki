# The radio

**[The cabinet](../bodies/cabinet.md) plays a radio station when it has been left alone long
enough** ([0260](../../raw/rules/0260-the-cabinet-plays-a-radio-station-when-left-alone.md)). It is a state of the machine like any other and not a screensaver: a slower
version of the same light show, the station's name across
[the four score displays](../things/player-displays.md), and `RADIO` on
[`MATCH`](../things/match-display.md) — or **`NO NET` while it is failing to connect** ([0260](../../raw/rules/0260-the-cabinet-plays-a-radio-station-when-left-alone.md)).

**You get there three ways: wait, hold the right flipper alone for nine seconds in attract mode, or
use `RADIO TEST` in [the service menu](the-service-menu.md)** ([0261](../../raw/rules/0261-in-the-radio-every-button-does-one-thing.md)). Once there, **every button
does one thing** ([0261](../../raw/rules/0261-in-the-radio-every-button-does-one-thing.md)):

| Button | Does |
|---|---|
| start | starts a game, exactly as it would from attract |
| right flipper | **the next station**, wrapping — it plays at once and its name comes up |
| left flipper, **tapped** | back to attract mode |
| left flipper, **held** | **the volume** — hold and the number climbs; let go and that is the volume |
| both flippers, nine seconds | the service menu, the same gesture as everywhere else |

*(the table is cabinet.md §2's, rendered for a player ([0261](../../raw/rules/0261-in-the-radio-every-button-does-one-thing.md)))*

**Stepping stations is how you audition them** ([0262](../../raw/rules/0262-stepping-stations-is-how-you-audition-them.md)). The name on the glass is the name in the
settings, so a station that will not play tells you exactly which entry to go and fix. **Each press
is saved immediately rather than on the way out** — this is the state a cabinet stands in for days,
and a choice only written when you leave would not survive somebody pulling the plug ([0262](../../raw/rules/0262-stepping-stations-is-how-you-audition-them.md)).

**The volume goes up in fives, one step a second, and it wraps** ([0263](../../raw/rules/0263-the-volume-goes-up-in-fives-and-wraps.md)). Hold the left flipper:
after a second the score displays change to `VOLUME` over a number and it climbs — 55, 60, 65 —
until you let go. **Past 100 the next step is 0**, so one button reaches every level and
overshooting only costs another lap; twenty-one seconds is the whole way round ([0263](../../raw/rules/0263-the-volume-goes-up-in-fives-and-wraps.md)).

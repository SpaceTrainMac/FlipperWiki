# Rule 0062 — Every ball that ends is counted out lamp by lamp

**At the glass: when your ball goes, the machine goes quiet and walks the lamps out one at a time.
That is the bonus being paid** ([0062](../../raw/rules/0062-every-ball-that-ends-is-counted-out-lamp.md)). Pages: [the end of a ball](../moments/end-of-ball.md).

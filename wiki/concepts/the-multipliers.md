# The three multipliers

**Three independent pairs of `DOUBLE` / `TRIPLE` lamps, and each multiplies its own collection and
nothing else** ([0038](../../raw/rules/0038-three-independent-pairs-of-double-triple-lamps-and.md)).

| Multiplier | Earned by | Multiplies |
|---|---|---|
| **bottom** | the 40th and 50th total bank completion, collected at [the right captive ball](../things/right-captive-ball.md) | [the bonus count](the-bonus-count.md) — 5 000 a bonus, up to 39 |
| **left hole** | the 2nd and 3rd successful left-ladder redemption | [the left ladder](the-hole-bonus.md)'s rungs and armed marker |
| **right hole** | the 2nd and 3rd successful right-ladder redemption | the right ladder's rungs and armed marker |

*(the table is rule 0038's, rendered for a player)*

See [the multiplier lamps](../things/multiplier-lamps.md).

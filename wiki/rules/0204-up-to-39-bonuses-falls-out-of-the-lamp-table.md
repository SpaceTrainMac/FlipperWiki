# Rule 0204 — The flyer's *Up to 39 Bonuses* falls out of the lamp table

**At the glass: 39 is not a designer's round number — it is nine unit lamps and three Super Bonus
lamps worth 10, 20 and 30** ([0204](../../raw/rules/0204-up-to-39-bonuses-falls-out-of-the-lamp-table.md)). Add them and the flyer's headline figure falls out, which
means the cap on [the bonus count](../concepts/the-bonus-count.md) is the ladder's own arithmetic
([0204](../../raw/rules/0204-up-to-39-bonuses-falls-out-of-the-lamp-table.md)). Pages: [the bonus count](../concepts/the-bonus-count.md).

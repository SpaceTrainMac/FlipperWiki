# Rule 0231 — The bonus and the match take about 43 seconds

**At the glass: the waiting is accurate, which does not make it short** ([0231](../../raw/rules/0231-the-bonus-and-the-match-take-about-43-seconds.md)). The count and the
transfer run at the cabinet's own speed and the ball is in the trough for all of it; watched on a
screen it passes, and with a ball in your hand it is a long time to stand still ([0231](../../raw/rules/0231-the-bonus-and-the-match-take-about-43-seconds.md)). Pages:
[the end of a turn](../moments/end-of-turn.md), [the Visual Pinball table](../bodies/vpx.md).

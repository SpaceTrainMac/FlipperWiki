# Rule 0201 — Pricing card — verbatim

**At the glass: the left-hand card is prices, and the half of it that still matters is the bottom
half** ([0201](../../raw/rules/0201-pricing-card-verbatim.md)). Three lines set coin values; the other three say that an extra ball and both free
plays are *indicated on display*, which is why those three announcements live on
[the backbox](../things/indicator-leds.md) rather than on the playfield ([0201](../../raw/rules/0201-pricing-card-verbatim.md)). Pages:
[the two cards on the apron](../things/apron-cards.md),
[the sixteen indicator LEDs](../things/indicator-leds.md).

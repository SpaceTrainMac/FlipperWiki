# Rule 0290 — `BONUS_COUNT_STEP`

**At the glass: this is the sound of being paid, one lamp at a time** ([0290](../../raw/rules/0290-cue-bonus-count-step.md)). A quiet ball is
8 steps and a full one 24, at 200 ms apart — **so it is the one cue that has to be short and must
not stack** ([0290](../../raw/rules/0290-cue-bonus-count-step.md)). Pages: [what the machine says](../concepts/the-sounds.md), [the end of a ball](../moments/end-of-ball.md).

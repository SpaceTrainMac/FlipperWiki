# Rule 0260 — The cabinet plays a radio station when it has been left alone

**At the glass: a slow light show, a station name across the four score displays and `RADIO` on
`MATCH`** — that is the machine in its radio state, not asleep ([0260](../../raw/rules/0260-the-cabinet-plays-a-radio-station-when-left-alone.md)). `NO NET` there means it
is failing to connect ([0260](../../raw/rules/0260-the-cabinet-plays-a-radio-station-when-left-alone.md)). Pages: [the radio](../moments/the-radio.md).

[machine.md § 1.5 The instruction cards - Rule card - verbatim](../../../FlipperArchitecture/docs/machine.md#rule-card--verbatim)

The three captive balls split 2 + 1 exactly as the switch matrix does: the two **twins**
(`BALLWITHBALL_LEFT_TWIN`, `BALLWITHBALL_RIGHT_TWIN`) are the card's *"center balls"*, and
`BALLWITHBALL_RIGHT_TOP` is its *"right ball"*.

# Rule 0243 — The keyboard accelerators are on the page, in the *keys* panel

**At the glass: look in the *keys* panel rather than for a table of keys** — the list is
deliberately kept in one place, because a copy of it would go stale the first time a switch was
renamed ([0243](../../raw/rules/0243-the-keyboard-accelerators-are-on-the-page.md)). Pages: [the browser simulator](../bodies/simulator.md).

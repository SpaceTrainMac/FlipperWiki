[gameplay.md § 4. The advance-bonus bank, and the combination](../../../FlipperArchitecture/docs/gameplay.md#4-the-advance-bonus-bank-and-the-combination)

**Each target, on its first hit, pays 100 and lights its own lamp.** A second hit on a target
already lit does nothing. Two targets struck in the same instant both count. **Hitting a target does
not move the bonus count by itself** — that waits for the bank to complete.

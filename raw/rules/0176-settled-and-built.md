**Settled, and built.** Forty sound cues exist in [FlipperSounds](../../../FlipperSounds), eleven of
them added on 2026-09-04 for the rules above — M-A-C's paying lanes ([§3](../../../FlipperArchitecture/docs/gameplay.md#3-m-a-c)), the extra bonus
([§7](../../../FlipperArchitecture/docs/gameplay.md#7-the-three-multipliers)) and the two the flyer names among them. This section used to say
*not settled*: twenty-nine cues, three rules calling for one, and the space-engine warp and the steam
train named here and in no sound set. **Which** cue speaks for which rule, and when, is
[FlipperEngine/docs/sound.md §4](../../../FlipperEngine/docs/sound.md#4-the-catalogue) and nowhere
else.

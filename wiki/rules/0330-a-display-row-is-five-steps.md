# Rule 0330 — A display row is five steps of the animation, not one

**At the glass: the line takes its time through the digits** — six shapes to cross one digit,
with everything else held still ([0330](../../raw/rules/0330-a-display-row-is-five-steps.md)). Pages: [attract mode](../moments/attract-mode.md), [what the glass shows](../concepts/what-the-glass-shows.md).

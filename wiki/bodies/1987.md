# The 1987 machine

**One of the four bodies is not a machine anybody can walk up to: it is what SPACE TRAIN did in
1987**, and every sentence on this page was read off photographs and a sales flyer rather than off
any code ([0182](../../raw/rules/0182-the-game-as-the-machine-tells-it.md)). That is the whole of its authority and the whole of its limit — where a page
about *this* machine and a sentence here disagree, the original said one thing and the cabinet
does another, on purpose.

**SPACE TRAIN was built in 1987 by MAC — *Máquinas Automáticas Computerizadas S.A.*, of Madrid**,
and the machine says so twice: the apron is printed `© 1987 MAC, S.A. ALL RIGHT RESERVED`, and the
relay board in the backbox is stencilled `SPACE TRAIN MAC / SYSTEM IV / A-01717` ([0183](../../raw/rules/0183-space-train-built-1987-by-mac-madrid.md)). Those
two markings are the only place the machine names itself, and everything else about *this* title
had to be read off paper.

**It is not really Space Train's cabinet: it is a MacPinBall System IV chassis with Space Train
dropped into it** ([0184](../../raw/rules/0184-a-macpinball-system-iv-machine-sold-under-multigame.md)). MAC sold System IV under a *MULTIGAME* scheme, where a new title meant
changing the playfield, the backglass and the game EPROM and nothing else — so the switch matrix
and the lamp matrix this machine carries are the *chassis'*, not this game's, which is why
[several switches](../things/every-switch.md) and [several lamps](../things/playfield-lamps.md) have
no role in Space Train at all ([0184](../../raw/rules/0184-a-macpinball-system-iv-machine-sold-under-multigame.md)).

| Manufacturer | MAC S.A., Madrid |

*(the table is machine.md §1.1's, rendered for a player ([0184](../../raw/rules/0184-a-macpinball-system-iv-machine-sold-under-multigame.md)))*

**Four of the things the flyer boasts about are not Space Train's at all — they are System IV's**,
sold with every machine MAC made ([0185](../../raw/rules/0185-behaviours-the-flyer-attributes-to-all-macpinball-machines.md)): **automatic percentaging**, where the machine adjusts by
itself how freely it hands out extra balls and free plays; **2 extra plays at high score**;
**Info-Speech**, a speaking announcer; and **1–4 players**. Read that list next to the playfield
and it says which of the machine's habits came in the box and which were designed for this title.

**Two of those four are why the backbox is lettered the way it is.** The `EXTRA PLAYS` legend and
[the eye under it](../things/extra-plays-eye.md) are the *2 extra plays at high score* the flyer
promises, and [what the machine says](../concepts/the-sounds.md) is what became of Info-Speech
([0185](../../raw/rules/0185-behaviours-the-flyer-attributes-to-all-macpinball-machines.md)). Neither means today what the flyer meant.

## Where it all comes from

**Eleven flyer scans and photographs, and four of them carry everything on this page** ([0186](../../raw/rules/0186-source-material.md)):

| The source | Shows | Worth |
|---|---|---|
| `FOTO06` | **the apron, with both instruction cards** | the highest of all — it is where both cards are read from |
| `FOTO04` | the **upper** playfield — M-A-C lanes, the bank, the bumpers | high |
| `FOTO05` | the **lower** playfield — the bonus ladder, Super Bonus, the multipliers | high |
| `FLYER01` / `FLYER02` | the feature list in English and Spanish, and the labelled playfield diagram | high |

*(the four rows are the ones machine.md §1.2 marks **High** or **Highest**; the other seven — the
backglass, the cabinet, the wiring — carry nothing about how the game is played ([0186](../../raw/rules/0186-source-material.md)))*

**Those photographs are 800 × 600, which is just short of enough** ([0187](../../raw/rules/0187-the-photographs-are-800-x-600-at-the-edge-of-legibility.md)). The instruction card
sits at the edge of legibility in them, so a character can be genuinely undecidable — and the rule
that follows from that is a habit rather than a fact: **anything resting on a marginal pixel is
flagged where it is claimed**, rather than smoothed over ([0187](../../raw/rules/0187-the-photographs-are-800-x-600-at-the-edge-of-legibility.md)). One character on the rule card
is flagged that way, and it is a value.

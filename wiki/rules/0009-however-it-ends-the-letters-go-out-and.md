# Rule 0009 — However it ends, the letters go out and M-A-C can be spelled again

**At the glass: when the flashing stops — extra ball won or window missed — the three letters go
dark and you start spelling again. M-A-C comes round as often as you can spell it** ([0009](../../raw/rules/0009-however-it-ends-the-letters-go-out-and.md)). Pages:
[M-A-C](../concepts/m-a-c.md), [the M, A and C lanes](../things/m-a-c-lanes.md),
[the upper target bank](../things/upper-target-bank.md).

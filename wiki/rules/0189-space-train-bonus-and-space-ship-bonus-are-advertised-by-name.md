# Rule 0189 — Space Train Bonus and Space Ship Bonus are advertised by name

**At the glass: these two are the only awards on the machine whose meaning was decided rather than
found.** The flyer names both, no 1987 source explains either, and no lamp on the playfield carries
either name — so what they are now is [the three multipliers reaching ×3](../concepts/flyer-bonuses.md),
which is an answer this cabinet gave and not one the sources handed over ([0189](../../raw/rules/0189-space-train-bonus-and-space-ship-bonus-are-advertised-by-name.md)). Pages:
[Space Ship Bonus and Space Train Bonus](../concepts/flyer-bonuses.md).

[gameplay.md § 7. The three multipliers](../../../FlipperArchitecture/docs/gameplay.md#7-the-three-multipliers)

**Space Train Bonus costs more than anything else on this machine.** ×3 on the bottom is fifty bank
completions — two hundred target hits at the very least — plus a shot at the right captive ball
inside a 20-second window; ×3 on a hole side is three redemptions, eighteen hits on that twin
([§6](../../../FlipperArchitecture/docs/gameplay.md#6-the-hole-bonuses-and-the-captive-balls)); and it wants all three. That is **236 scoring
hits and two windowed collections**, spread across a game rather than a ball, and it is the only
thing on the machine that asks a player to finish all three ladders.

# Rule 0119 — `BUNKER_RIGHT`

**Pays:** 100. **Advances:** —. **Collects:** an **extra ball** when either hole ladder is past rung 2, once a ball; the right ladder's five rungs and marker, if that side's 30 s window is open; a right marker armed with no window running, at any time. ([0119](../../raw/rules/0119-bunker_right.md))

It is explained on [right bunker](../things/right-bunker.md).

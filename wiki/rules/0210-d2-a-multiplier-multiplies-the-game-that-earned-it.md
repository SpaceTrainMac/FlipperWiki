# Rule 0210 — D2: a multiplier multiplies the game that earned it, and nothing else

**At the glass: this is why three `TRIPLE`s do not stack.** The flyer advertises *Double & Triple*
and nothing beyond ×3, so the three pairs were made to multiply their own collections only —
composing them reaches ×27, which is not a number this machine can mean, and multiplying a combined
step score had one ladder's `DOUBLE` quietly paying out on bumper points ([0210](../../raw/rules/0210-d2-a-multiplier-multiplies-the-game-that-earned-it.md)). Pages:
[the three multipliers](../concepts/the-multipliers.md),
[decided where the sources are silent](../concepts/where-the-sources-are-silent.md).

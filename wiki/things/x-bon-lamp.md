# The X BONUS lamp

**`X_BON` flashing means a 20-second window on the extra bonus, and it opens at your 40th and 50th
completion of the `ADVANCE BONUS` bank** ([0019](../../raw/rules/0019-four-awards-are-lit-on-a-timer-instead.md)). Those are the only two times it opens, and the
count that reaches them is one of the things the machine tracks and shows nowhere else ([0011](../../raw/rules/0011-ten-quantities-and-everything-below-is-one-of.md)). **It
is a total across the game, not this ball's ladder** — it never resets, so the 40th completion can
land on any ball ([0026](../../raw/rules/0026-a-separate-hidden-count-tracks-bank-completions-across.md)).

TBC — the *1987* photograph of the lower playfield shows **an `X BONUS` target** ([0196](../../raw/rules/0196-lower-playfield-foto05.md)), and
this machine has no switch of that name: the four
[`ADVANCE BONUS` targets](advance-bonus-targets.md) are the only targets in the matrix, and
`X_BON` is a lamp ([0109](../../raw/rules/0109-every-one-of-the-48-and-what-a.md)). Whether the
photograph shows a target that was never wired, or a lamp read as a target, only the cabinet can
settle.

**What is in the window is [the extra bonus](../concepts/the-extra-bonus.md)**: hit
[the right captive ball](right-captive-ball.md) inside it for a flat 500 000 and the bottom
multiplier — `X2_BOT` at the 40th, `X3_BOT` at the 50th ([0039](../../raw/rules/0039-the-bottom-pair-is-x_bons-doing.md)). See
[lit awards](../concepts/lit-awards.md) and [the advance-bonus bank](../concepts/advance-bonus-bank.md).

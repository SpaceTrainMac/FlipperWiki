**Nothing shows the count as a number.** The two-digit field that might have is the extra-ball
counter, so a player at 25 reads five ladder lamps and two `SUPER_BON`s and adds them — which is
what the lamps are laid out to be read as, and is the same arithmetic the backlight is doing in
colour ([§13](../../../FlipperArchitecture/docs/gameplay.md#13-what-the-machine-shows)).

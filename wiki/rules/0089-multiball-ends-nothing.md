# Rule 0089 — Multiball ends nothing

**At the glass: losing one of the two balls costs you nothing — no count, no clearing. The turn ends
when both are home** ([0089](../../raw/rules/0089-multiball-ends-nothing.md)). Pages: [multiball](../concepts/multiball.md).

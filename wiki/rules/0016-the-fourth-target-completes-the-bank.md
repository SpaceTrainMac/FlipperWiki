# Rule 0016 — The fourth target completes the bank

**At the glass: the fourth target is worth 10 000, a rung on the bonus ladder and a stage of the
combination — and the four lamps flashing three times is the machine telling you so.** Then the bank
is empty and you can do it again: the second time lights the right `EXTRA BALL`, the third lights
`SPECIAL` and hands you an extra ball outright ([0016](../../raw/rules/0016-the-fourth-target-completes-the-bank.md)). Pages:

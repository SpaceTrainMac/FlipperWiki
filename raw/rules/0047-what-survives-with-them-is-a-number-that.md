**What survives with them is a number that gets large.** A ×3 bottom multiplier held from ball 1
means a bonus count of 39 is worth 585 000 on each of the remaining balls, and
[§13](../../../FlipperArchitecture/docs/gameplay.md#13-what-the-machine-shows)'s `×10` indicator is the consequence.

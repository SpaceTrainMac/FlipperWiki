# The extra bonus

**A flat 500 000, and the only way to it is the `X BONUS` window.** `X_BON` lights and flashes for a
configurable 20 seconds the moment your total bank completions reach 40; hitting
[the right captive ball](../things/right-captive-ball.md) inside that window releases it — its own
sound and light, 500 000, and `X2_BOT` lights ([0039](../../raw/rules/0039-the-bottom-pair-is-x_bons-doing.md)).

**The same happens again at the 50th completion**, and a collection inside that second window lights
`X3_BOT` instead — the ceiling, whether or not `X2_BOT` was ever lit ([0039](../../raw/rules/0039-the-bottom-pair-is-x_bons-doing.md)).

**A window that closes uncollected costs both the 500 000 and the multiplier** ([0039](../../raw/rules/0039-the-bottom-pair-is-x_bons-doing.md)).

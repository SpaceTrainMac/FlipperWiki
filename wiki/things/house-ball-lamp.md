# The HOUSE BALL lamp

**`HOUSE_BALL` flashing is drain protection, and it runs for 15 seconds** ([0019](../../raw/rules/0019-four-awards-are-lit-on-a-timer-instead.md)). It is the
shortest of the machine's four timed windows.

**Each formula tops out at exactly 15, the channel's own maximum, at that ladder's own maximum.** A
bonus count of 39 is 9 units plus three lit `SUPER_BON`s, `9 + 2 × 3 = 15`; five rungs at `TRIPLE`
is `5 + 5 + 5 = 15`.

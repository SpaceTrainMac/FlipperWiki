# Rule 0020 — Steady means waiting, flashing means hurrying

**At the glass: one glance tells you whether you have time.** A steady lamp is an award that will
wait until the ball ends; a flashing one is a window closing. Every duration is configurable, so the
seconds on this machine may not be the seconds on another ([0020](../../raw/rules/0020-steady-means-waiting-flashing-means-hurrying.md)). Pages:
[lit awards](../concepts/lit-awards.md).

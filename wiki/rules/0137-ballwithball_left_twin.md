# Rule 0137 — `BALLWITHBALL_LEFT_TWIN`

**Pays:** —. **Advances:** the **left** hole ladder by one rung, or opens/redeems its 30 s window at the 5th/6th hit. **Collects:** the left ladder's five rungs and marker, if that side's window is open. ([0137](../../raw/rules/0137-ballwithball_left_twin.md))

It is explained on [centre captive balls](../things/centre-captive-balls.md).

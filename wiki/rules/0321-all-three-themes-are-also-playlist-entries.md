# Rule 0321 — All three themes are also playlist entries

**At the glass: nothing interrupts a theme except the same theme coming round again** — all three
sit in the jukebox's own playlist as well as being cues ([0321](../../raw/rules/0321-all-three-themes-are-also-playlist-entries.md)). Pages:

# Rule 0205 — With `BONUS X 5000` printed on the playfield

**At the glass: the biggest number the bonus count can reach was worked out in 1987 from two things
painted on the playfield** — `BONUS X 5000` down the ladder and the `DOUBLE` / `TRIPLE` beside it.
39 × 5 000 is 195 000, doubled 390 000, tripled **585 000** ([0205](../../raw/rules/0205-with-bonus-x-5000-printed-on-the-playfield.md)). Pages:
[the bonus count](../concepts/the-bonus-count.md).

# Rule 0317 — These name *when* they play

**At the glass: a theme starting means the song you were listening to is gone** — the jukebox is
cut, and when the theme ends it starts the *next* entry rather than resuming ([0317](../../raw/rules/0317-music-cues-name-when-they-play.md)). The three
music cues are named for their moment rather than their content ([0317](../../raw/rules/0317-music-cues-name-when-they-play.md)). Pages:
[what the machine says](../concepts/the-sounds.md).

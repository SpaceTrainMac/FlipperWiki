# Rule 0116 — `A`

**Pays:** —. **Advances:** lights or clears the `A` lamp; **inert while its own window runs**. **Collects:** when M-A-C is complete, opens a 20 s window on the **upper** `EXTRA BALL` lamp and ends the paying mode (§3). ([0116](../../raw/rules/0116-a.md))

It is explained on [m a c lanes](../things/m-a-c-lanes.md).

# OUTFIRE — the start ramp's exit

**It sees a served ball leave the ramp**, which is what times
[multiball](../concepts/multiball.md)'s 2.5-second hold ([0056](../../raw/rules/0056-bunker_left-pays-100-and-starts-multiball-if-a.md)).

**It pays 1 000 while the left bunker is waiting, and nothing at any other time** — the machine
paying for the second ball arriving rather than for anything you did with it ([0059](../../raw/rules/0059-outfire-pays-1-000-while-the-left-bunker.md)).

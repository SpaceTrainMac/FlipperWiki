# Rule 0055 — Two saucers, each with two jobs

**At the glass: the two holes upper centre each swallow the ball and kick it out again. Each is one
side's hole bonus, and each has a second job of its own** ([0055](../../raw/rules/0055-two-saucers-each-its-own-sides-hole-as.md)). Pages:
[the left bunker](../things/left-bunker.md), [the right bunker](../things/right-bunker.md).

# Rule 0242 — Sound is off unless the settings say `"sound": true`

**At the glass: a silent page is not a page that is missing the cues** — the music and the effects
are drawn as they are raised whether or not anything is audible ([0242](../../raw/rules/0242-sound-is-off-unless-the-settings-say-so.md)). A game that draws no
effects at all is an engine older than 2026-09-08, not a broken instrument ([0242](../../raw/rules/0242-sound-is-off-unless-the-settings-say-so.md)). Pages:
[the browser simulator](../bodies/simulator.md), [what the machine says](../concepts/the-sounds.md).

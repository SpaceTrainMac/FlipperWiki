# Rule 0213 — What none of these touch is the award chain the card describes

**At the glass: the four decisions pick numbers, and none of them invents a rule.** `SPECIAL`, the
combination's two extra balls, the right ball's 30 000 and the end-of-ball bonus are a chain of
behaviour rather than a set of values, and they were designed in their own right rather than
assumed here ([0213](../../raw/rules/0213-what-none-of-these-touch-is-the-award-chain.md)). Pages:
[decided where the sources are silent](../concepts/where-the-sources-are-silent.md).

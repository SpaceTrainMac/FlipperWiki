# Rule 0289 — `MAIN_BONUS_ONE_LIGHT`

**At the glass: the ladder is full and will not move again this ball** ([0289](../../raw/rules/0289-cue-main-bonus-one-light.md)). It replaces the
ordinary advance cue at the cap, so the machine says *full* rather than saying *moved* one last
time ([0289](../../raw/rules/0289-cue-main-bonus-one-light.md)). Pages: [what the machine says](../concepts/the-sounds.md), [the bonus count](../concepts/the-bonus-count.md).

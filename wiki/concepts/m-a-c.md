# M-A-C

**Once the three letters are lit, the two outer lanes pay and keep paying.** `M` and `C` are worth
30 000 each, and taking one does not spend it: a player who can keep finding the two outer lanes is
paid 30 000 every time ([0001](../../raw/rules/0001-m-and-c-pay-for-as-long-as-a-is-left-alone.md)).

**`A` is the lane that ends it, and what it buys is a chance at an extra ball.** It closes the
sequence, so *when to take `A`* is the only real decision M-A-C asks of a player
([0001](../../raw/rules/0001-m-and-c-pay-for-as-long-as-a-is-left-alone.md)). Taking it opens a
20-second window on the upper `EXTRA BALL` lamp: that lamp and the `A` lamp flash together, and the
right captive ball hit inside the window awards the extra ball. If the window closes first, nothing
is awarded ([0002](../../raw/rules/0002-a-opens-a-20-second-window-on-the-upper-extra-ball.md)).

**So the shape of a cycle is: milk `M` and `C`, then spend the sequence on `A` and go for the right
captive ball.** The 20 seconds are configurable
([0002](../../raw/rules/0002-a-opens-a-20-second-window-on-the-upper-extra-ball.md)).

**Collecting it.** While M-A-C is complete the three lanes stop spelling and start paying, each with
its own sound cue and light effect:

| Lane | Pays | Lights |
|---|---|---|
| `M` | **30 000**, and can be taken again | `30K_TOP_L` |
| `C` | **30 000**, and can be taken again | `30K_TOP_R` |
| `A` | opens the extra-ball window below — and ends the paying mode | `EXTRA_BALL_TOP` |

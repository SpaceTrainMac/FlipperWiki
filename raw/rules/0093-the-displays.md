| Display | During a game |
|---|---|
| the four 6-digit player scores | that player's total **as it stood when their turn began**; it moves only during a transfer |
| the fifth 6-digit display, `MATCH` | the **turn score** — zero when the turn begins, blanked when it ends |
| `BALL IN PLAY` | **turns left, counting down**, including the one being played: 3, 2, 1 — and configurable, since four and five are settings the cabinet takes |
| the two-digit field | **extra balls in hand** — one up per award, one down per drain |
| `EXTRA PLAYS` | a `0` that winks — an eye, not a number |
| the two digits at 153 and 155 | **dark** — the backglass artwork covers them |

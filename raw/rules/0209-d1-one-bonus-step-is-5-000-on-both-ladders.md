| **D1** | **One bonus step is 5 000, on both hole-bonus ladders.** The left one awarded 1 000 | The lower playfield prints `BONUS X 5000` vertically down the main ladder — the only printed statement of what a bonus step is worth. The right-hand ladder's lamps are named *"Bonus x 5000 rechts"* on the spreadsheet, and the two sides are otherwise the same mechanism: same captive-ball twin, five rungs, 30 000 to collect, ×2 then ×3. **A machine paying 1 000 for one and 5 000 for the other, for the same action and the same payoff, would be teaching the player to ignore one side of its own playfield.** The only thing that ever suggested 1 000 is that the left ladder's spreadsheet name carries no value — an absence, against three presences |

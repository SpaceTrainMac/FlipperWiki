# Rule 0237 — The three kinds of control, and why they look different

**At the glass: the picture tells you how to work each control before you touch it** — most
switches are momentary, five carry a blue ring and toggle because a ball could rest on them, and
three are drawn apart because they are cabinet buttons that close on the way down and open on the
way up ([0237](../../raw/rules/0237-the-three-kinds-of-control.md)). Pages: [the browser simulator](../bodies/simulator.md).

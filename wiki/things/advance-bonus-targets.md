# The four ADVANCE BONUS targets

**Four targets low on the playfield, and hitting all four is the machine's only three-stage award**
([0014](../../raw/rules/0014-four-advance-bonus-targets-on-the-lower-playfield.md)).

**Each target pays 100 the first time it is hit and lights its own lamp; hitting it again does
nothing.** Two struck in the same instant both count ([0015](../../raw/rules/0015-each-target-on-its-first-hit-pays-100.md)). **All four down and the lamps flash three times, then go out and the bank starts again from empty**
([0016](../../raw/rules/0016-the-fourth-target-completes-the-bank.md)). What a completion is worth is [the advance-bonus bank](../concepts/advance-bonus-bank.md).

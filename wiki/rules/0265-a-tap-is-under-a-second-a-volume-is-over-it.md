# Rule 0265 — A tap is under a second, a volume is over it

**At the glass: the number appearing is the line.** Release before it and you are back in attract;
release after it and you have set a volume and stayed. Reaching for both flippers abandons the
volume and opens the service menu ([0265](../../raw/rules/0265-a-tap-is-under-a-second-a-volume-is-over-it.md)). Pages: [the radio](../moments/the-radio.md).

# Rule 0253 — After three seconds the light show stops and a joke comes up

**At the glass: three seconds in, the lights stop and the displays make a joke** — that is the
acknowledgement, and the thing to wait for when you are not sure the hold is registering ([0253](../../raw/rules/0253-after-three-seconds-a-joke-comes-up.md)).
Let go there and attract mode simply carries on ([0253](../../raw/rules/0253-after-three-seconds-a-joke-comes-up.md)). Pages:
[the service menu](../moments/the-service-menu.md).

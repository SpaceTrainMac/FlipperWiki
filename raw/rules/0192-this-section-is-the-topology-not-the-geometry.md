**And this section is the topology, not the geometry.** It says what connects to what, off the
flyer; **where any of it actually is** — the deck, the routes, the posts, 42 in the inventory, the two
wire rails, the plastics — is [geometry.md](../../../FlipperArchitecture/docs/geometry.md)'s subject. [topography.json](../../../FlipperArchitecture/docs/topography.json),
read off the photographs in [../photos/cabinet/](../../../FlipperArchitecture/photos/cabinet/annotations.md) and written and checked
by [../playfield/gen_topography.py](../../../FlipperArchitecture/playfield/gen_topography.py), is the cabinet's evidence; since
2026-09-07 the table that plays is FlipperVPX's own `table/geometry/`, measured against it. The records
are [what-stands](../../../FlipperArchitecture/docs/concepts/done/20260905-what-stands-on-the-playfield.md) and
[somebody-elses-table](../../../FlipperArchitecture/docs/concepts/done/20260907-somebody-elses-table.md). Nothing below is a position.

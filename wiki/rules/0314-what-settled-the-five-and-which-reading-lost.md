# Rule 0314 — What settled the five

**At the glass: five of the forty could have meant something else, and what they nearly meant is
written down** ([0314](../../raw/rules/0314-what-settled-the-five-and-which-reading-lost.md)). Each was settled by a rule of the game rather than by taste, and the
losing reading is kept because a cue settled twice will settle a third way ([0314](../../raw/rules/0314-what-settled-the-five-and-which-reading-lost.md)). Pages:
[what the machine says](../concepts/the-sounds.md).

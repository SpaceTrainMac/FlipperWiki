[gameplay.md § 11. The end of a turn: the transfer](../../../FlipperArchitecture/docs/gameplay.md#11-the-end-of-a-turn-the-transfer)

**Nothing is announced during a ball.** A player's own total does not move while they are playing,
so there is nothing to cross until the transfer; the announcement lands in the gap between turns, in
the middle of a two-second count-up, as punctuation rather than as an interruption.

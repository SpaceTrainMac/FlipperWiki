# Rule 0139 — `BALLWITHBALL_RIGHT_TOP`

**Pays:** **500 000** if `X_BON` is flashing. **Advances:** lights **both** `HOLE BONUS 30000` lamps; lights `X2_BOT` or `X3_BOT` if `X_BON` is flashing. **Collects:** the right `EXTRA BALL` when it is lit (2nd bank completion); the **upper** `EXTRA BALL` inside `A`'s 20 s window. ([0139](../../raw/rules/0139-ballwithball_right_top.md))

It is explained on [right captive ball](../things/right-captive-ball.md).

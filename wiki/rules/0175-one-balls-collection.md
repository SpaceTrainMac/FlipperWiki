# Rule 0175 — One ball's collection

**585 000 + 2 × 165 000 = 915 000**, which is the ceiling on what a ball collects at the end of it
([0175](../../raw/rules/0175-one-balls-collection.md)) — and not the ceiling on what a ball is worth ([0067](../../raw/rules/0067-915-000-is-the-ceiling-on-what-a.md)). It is a row of
[every award, in one table](../concepts/every-award.md).

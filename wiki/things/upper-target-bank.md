# The upper target bank — 30.000 · EXTRA BALL · 30.000

**The two `30.000` lamps light when `M` and `C` pay, and the `EXTRA BALL` lamp when `A` is
taken** — each lane, with M-A-C complete, lights the lamp the playfield has an arrow painted to,
with its own sound cue and light effect: `M` lights `30K_TOP_L`, `C` lights `30K_TOP_R`, `A`
lights `EXTRA_BALL_TOP` ([0006](../../raw/rules/0006-collecting-it.md)).

**The upper `EXTRA BALL` lamp flashing is a 20-second invitation to hit the right captive ball.**
It opens when `A` is taken with M-A-C complete; `EXTRA_BALL_TOP` and the `A` lamp flash together,
the [right captive ball](right-captive-ball.md) inside the window awards the extra ball, and if the
window closes first nothing is awarded
([0002](../../raw/rules/0002-a-opens-a-20-second-window-on-the-upper-extra-ball.md)). The mechanic
behind it is [M-A-C](../concepts/m-a-c.md).

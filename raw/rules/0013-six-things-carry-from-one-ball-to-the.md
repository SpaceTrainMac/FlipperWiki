[gameplay.md § 1. What the machine keeps track of](../../../FlipperArchitecture/docs/gameplay.md#1-what-the-machine-keeps-track-of)

**Six things carry from one ball to the next and everything else is cleared**: the three
multipliers, the total bank-completions count that feeds the bottom one, the two hole-redemption
counts that feed the other two, the three M-A-C letters ([§3](../../../FlipperArchitecture/docs/gameplay.md#3-m-a-c)), the count of extra balls
won, and the turn score. A ball begins with the bonus count at zero, both hole ladders empty, no
marker armed, and no award lit.

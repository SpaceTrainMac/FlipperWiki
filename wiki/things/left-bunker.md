# The left bunker

**It has [a coil](the-coils.md) that kicks the ball back out**, and so does the right one ([0427](../../raw/rules/0427-the-nine-coils.md)).

**A saucer that swallows the ball and shoots it out again under coil power** — one of the flyer's
two *elevator holes*, its *2 shooter holes to upper level*, upper centre on the playfield ([0055](../../raw/rules/0055-two-saucers-each-its-own-sides-hole-as.md)).

**It pays 100, and it is what starts [multiball](../concepts/multiball.md)** — if a ball is staged
and ready to serve ([0056](../../raw/rules/0056-bunker_left-pays-100-and-starts-multiball-if-a.md)).

**During multiball it simply spits the ball back** — there is no third ball to serve ([0057](../../raw/rules/0057-the-machine-has-exactly-two-balls-not-the.md)).

**It is where the left hole-bonus ladder is redeemed, and that job does not care whether a ball was
cached.** A hit while the left window is blinking pays the five rungs and the marker together, at
the left multiplier, and resets the ladder ([0032](../../raw/rules/0032-redeeming-inside-the-window-takes-either-shot-the.md), [0058](../../raw/rules/0058-it-is-also-the-left-hole-ladders-redemption.md)) — the other shot that redeems it is the left
centre ball itself ([0032](../../raw/rules/0032-redeeming-inside-the-window-takes-either-shot-the.md)). **A left marker armed with no window running is collected here too, at
any time** ([0058](../../raw/rules/0058-it-is-also-the-left-hole-ladders-redemption.md)).

**All of that happens on top of starting multiball, not instead of it** — and when multiball does
start, the ball this hit put in the bunker leaves it the way multiball always ejects one: held until
the second ball leaves the ramp, then released 2.5 seconds later ([0058](../../raw/rules/0058-it-is-also-the-left-hole-ladders-redemption.md)).

See [the hole bonus](../concepts/the-hole-bonus.md) and [the right bunker](right-bunker.md).

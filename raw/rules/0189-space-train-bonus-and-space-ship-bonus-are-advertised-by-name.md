**Space Train Bonus** and **Space Ship Bonus** are advertised by name, explained nowhere in the 1987
sources, and match no lamp in [hardware.md](../../../FlipperArchitecture/docs/hardware.md). **They were the largest gap in the rules
and they are no longer one**: [gameplay.md §7](../../../FlipperArchitecture/docs/gameplay.md#the-two-the-flyer-names) gives both to
the three multipliers reaching ×3 — the Ship to `X3_BOT` alone, the Train to all three at once. That
is this cabinet answering a question the sources leave open, not a source being found.

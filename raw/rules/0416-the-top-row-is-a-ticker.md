**The top row is a ticker, and the names belong together.** The end of the string is followed
directly by the beginning of it, with no dark gap between DEUTSCHLANG and FRITZ — the separator
`CONTRIBUTORS` ends with is what stands between them. Nobody should have to arrive at the right
second to see all three.

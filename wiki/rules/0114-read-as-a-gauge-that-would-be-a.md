# Rule 0114 — The backlight is not a gauge, and is not meant to be read as one

**At the glass: do not read the colour as a number. The lamps are the number; the colour is the machine enjoying itself** ([0114](../../raw/rules/0114-read-as-a-gauge-that-would-be-a.md)). Pages: [the cabinet backlight](../things/cabinet-backlight.md).

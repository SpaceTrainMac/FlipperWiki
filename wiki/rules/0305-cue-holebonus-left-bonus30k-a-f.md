# Rule 0305 — `HOLEBONUS_LEFT_BONUS30K_A`

**At the glass: a redemption is not one sound, it is six** ([0305](../../raw/rules/0305-cue-holebonus-left-bonus30k-a-f.md)). Five rungs at 5 000 and the
marker at 30 000, all at that side's multiplier — 55 000 flat and 165 000 at ×3 — and **a
redemption that paid them to one sound would say less than the playfield does** ([0305](../../raw/rules/0305-cue-holebonus-left-bonus30k-a-f.md)). Pages: [what the machine says](../concepts/the-sounds.md), [the hole bonus](../concepts/the-hole-bonus.md).

# The slingshots

**They kick and they score nothing** ([0051](../../raw/rules/0051-the-two-slingshots-kick-and-score-nothing.md)). **They do flash [the cabinet backlight](cabinet-backlight.md) white on their own kick**, with no
lamp of their own to go with it — and they still score nothing ([0053](../../raw/rules/0053-a-bumper-or-a-slingshot-hit-also-flashes.md)).

**The two remaining sidebar switches do nothing at all** — not a score, not a lamp ([0051](../../raw/rules/0051-the-two-slingshots-kick-and-score-nothing.md)).

**They have a sound of their own even so** — the only cue on the machine attached to an event
that pays nothing ([what the machine says](../concepts/the-sounds.md)) ([0294](../../raw/rules/0294-cue-sidebars-bottom.md)).

**Real machines speed the bonus count up as it runs, and this one does not.** One pace is enough
until somebody has watched the 4.8-second case in [§10](../../../FlipperArchitecture/docs/gameplay.md#10-the-end-of-a-ball-the-count); a pace
that changes is much harder to reason about than one that does not.

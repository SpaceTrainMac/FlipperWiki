# Rule 0001 — M and C pay for as long as A is left alone

**At the glass: with all three letters lit, keep shooting the outer lanes — each is 30 000, every
time.** The middle lane, `A`, ends the paying and starts the next thing, so leave it alone until
you mean it ([0001](../../raw/rules/0001-m-and-c-pay-for-as-long-as-a-is-left-alone.md)). Pages: [M-A-C](../concepts/m-a-c.md),
[the M, A and C lanes](../things/m-a-c-lanes.md).

# Rule 0203 — Coin handling is out of scope by decision

**At the glass: the three coin lines on the apron buy nothing, and nothing is broken** ([0203](../../raw/rules/0203-coin-handling-is-out-of-scope-by-decision.md)).
Coin handling was left out deliberately — this cabinet stands in a house and every game on it is
free — which is what leaves [the `CREDITS` field](../things/status-row.md) with nothing of its own
to count ([0203](../../raw/rules/0203-coin-handling-is-out-of-scope-by-decision.md)). Pages: [the two cards on the apron](../things/apron-cards.md),
[the status row](../things/status-row.md).

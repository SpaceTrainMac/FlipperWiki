# Rule 0008 — While the window runs the three M-A-C lanes do nothing at all

**At the glass: once `A` has opened the window, rolling the top lanes does nothing for 20 seconds —
go for the right captive ball instead. Everything else on the playfield works as usual** ([0008](../../raw/rules/0008-while-the-window-runs-the-three-m-a-c-lanes.md)).

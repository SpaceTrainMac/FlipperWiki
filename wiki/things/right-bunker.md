# The right bunker

**The other of the two saucers** — swallowing the ball and shooting it out again under coil power,
the flyer's second *elevator hole*, upper centre ([0055](../../raw/rules/0055-two-saucers-each-its-own-sides-hole-as.md)).

**Up to three things happen when a ball lands in it** ([0060](../../raw/rules/0060-bunker_right-is-the-cards-hole-for-the-right.md)):

1. It pays **100**.
2. If either hole ladder is past rung 2, it awards an **extra ball** — once per ball.
3. If the right window is blinking, it redeems the five rungs and the marker together, at the right
   multiplier, and resets that ladder ([0032](../../raw/rules/0032-redeeming-inside-the-window-takes-either-shot-the.md), [0060](../../raw/rules/0060-bunker_right-is-the-cards-hole-for-the-right.md)). A right marker armed with no window running is
   collected here too, at any time ([0060](../../raw/rules/0060-bunker_right-is-the-cards-hole-for-the-right.md)).

**It kicks the ball back out after five seconds**, and the collection is two steps at most — the
extra ball and one redemption or marker — which fits inside that hold with room to spare ([0060](../../raw/rules/0060-bunker_right-is-the-cards-hole-for-the-right.md)).
The other shot that redeems the right ladder is the right centre ball itself ([0032](../../raw/rules/0032-redeeming-inside-the-window-takes-either-shot-the.md)).

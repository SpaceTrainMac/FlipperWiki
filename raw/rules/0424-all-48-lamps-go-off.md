[credits.md § CreditsMachine — On the way out](../../../FlipperEngine/docs/states/credits.md#on-the-way-out)

**All 48 lamps go off** — not the ones this machine lit, which it could not name anyway. A scatter of
lamps left burning is exactly what a playfield looks like with a game in progress, and the state
after this one is `PlayerSelect`, where there is none.

# Rule 0195 — Upper playfield (FOTO04)

**At the glass: the three lamps under the bumpers have arrows painted to them, and nothing behind
them** ([0195](../../raw/rules/0195-upper-playfield-foto04.md)). `M`, `A` and `C` are the only contacts up there; the `30.000` — `EXTRA BALL` —
`30.000` row is a display of what those three lanes pay. **Two blue-capped bumpers are visible and
the third's position is TBC**, and the left edge carries a numbered lane under the raised tunnel
with a five-step ladder and its own `TRIPLE` / `DOUBLE` ([0195](../../raw/rules/0195-upper-playfield-foto04.md)). Pages:
[the upper target bank](../things/upper-target-bank.md), [the three pop bumpers](../things/pop-bumpers.md),
[the two hole-bonus ladders](../things/hole-bonus-ladders.md).

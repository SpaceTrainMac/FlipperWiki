**Seven segments cost this one weak word.** `I` is the shape of a `1` and `O` the shape of a `0`,
and `R` is three segments — `TRAIN` is the least legible of the four. Nothing is missing from the
machine's character set; it is legibility and not capability, and the sound cue carries what the
digits cannot.

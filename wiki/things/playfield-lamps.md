# The 48 playfield lamps

**Every lamp on the playfield belongs to one of nine groups, and this is which** ([0109](../../raw/rules/0109-every-one-of-the-48-and-what-a.md)). Each row
links to the page that says what the group does.

| Lamps | Lit means |
|---|---|
| [the three M-A-C letters](m-a-c-lanes.md) | that letter is spelled; all three blinking is complete; the centre one flashing with the upper `EXTRA BALL` is `A`'s 20-second window |
| [the four `ADVANCE BONUS` lamps](advance-bonus-targets.md) | that target has been hit this bank; all four flash three times, then clear, on completion |
| [the nine ladder lamps and the three Super Bonus lamps](bonus-ladder.md) | the units and the tens of the bonus count |
| [`X2_BOT` / `X3_BOT`](multiplier-lamps.md) | the bonus count is doubled or tripled for the rest of the game; `X3_BOT` lighting is Space Ship Bonus |
| [the five rungs a side, and each side's `HOLE BONUS 30000`](hole-bonus-ladders.md) | that side's ladder; all six blinking is a 30-second window, a steady `30 000` is the right captive ball's arming |
| [each side's `DOUBLE` / `TRIPLE` pair](multiplier-lamps.md) | that hole bonus is doubled or tripled for the rest of the game |
| [`SPECIAL`, both](special-lamps.md) | either outlane pays 5 000 |
| [the two `EXTRA BALL` lamps and the two `30.000`](upper-target-bank.md) | an extra ball waiting at the right captive ball, and the `M` and `C` lanes paying |
| [the three bumper lamps](pop-bumpers.md), [`HOUSE_BALL`](house-ball-lamp.md), [`X_BON`](x-bon-lamp.md) | a bumper just fired; drain protection is open; the 40th or 50th completion's window is open |

*(the table is rule 0109's, rendered for a player)*

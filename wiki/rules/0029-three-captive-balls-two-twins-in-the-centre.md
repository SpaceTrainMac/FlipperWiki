# Rule 0029 — Three captive balls: two twins in the centre, one upper right

**At the glass: the two balls sitting side by side in the middle each run one side's hole bonus; the
one above and right of them runs neither and does something else entirely** ([0029](../../raw/rules/0029-three-captive-balls-two-twins-in-the-centre.md)). Pages:
[the two centre captive balls](../things/centre-captive-balls.md),
[the right captive ball](../things/right-captive-ball.md), [the hole bonus](../concepts/the-hole-bonus.md).

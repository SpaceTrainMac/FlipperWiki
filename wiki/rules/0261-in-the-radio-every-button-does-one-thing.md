# Rule 0261 — Every button does one thing

**At the glass: start plays a game, the right flipper changes station, a tap on the left goes back
to attract, a hold on the left sets the volume, and both together open the service menu** ([0261](../../raw/rules/0261-in-the-radio-every-button-does-one-thing.md)).
There are three ways into the state: wait, hold the right flipper alone, or use `RADIO TEST`
([0261](../../raw/rules/0261-in-the-radio-every-button-does-one-thing.md)). Pages: [the radio](../moments/the-radio.md).

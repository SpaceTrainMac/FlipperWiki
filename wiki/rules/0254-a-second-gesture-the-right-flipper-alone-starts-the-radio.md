# Rule 0254 — There is a second gesture on the same two buttons

**At the glass: two nine-second gestures share the flippers — both for the menu, the right one
alone for the radio — and they cannot be confused** ([0254](../../raw/rules/0254-a-second-gesture-the-right-flipper-alone-starts-the-radio.md)). A single-flipper hold only counts
while the other button is up, so a hand landing late does nothing and a hand slipping off mid-hold
does not become a radio request ([0254](../../raw/rules/0254-a-second-gesture-the-right-flipper-alone-starts-the-radio.md)). Pages: [the service menu](../moments/the-service-menu.md).

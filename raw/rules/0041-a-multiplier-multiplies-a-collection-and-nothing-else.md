**A multiplier multiplies a collection and nothing else.** The 100 a target pays, the 10 000 a bank
completion pays, the 30 000 an M-A-C lane pays and the 5 000 an inlane pays are all paid flat — and
so does the extra bonus's own 500 000, which is a flat collection in its own right rather than a
multiplied one.

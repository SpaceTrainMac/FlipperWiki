| # | Decided | From |
|---|---|---|
| **D2** | **A multiplier multiplies the game that earned it, and nothing else.** Multipliers do not compose | The machine advertises *"Double & Triple"*. Nine games multiplied together reach ×27, which is not a number this cabinet can mean, and the product applied to the *combined* step score — so a hole-bonus multiplier was silently multiplying bumper points. Multiplying each game's own score by its own multiplier keeps the ceiling at ×3 and stops one ladder's `DOUBLE` paying out on another's targets |

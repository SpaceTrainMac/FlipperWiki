# Rule 0191 — The two-level loop

**At the glass: the ball goes up three ways and comes down two, and the two sets do not match**
([0191](../../raw/rules/0191-the-two-level-loop-up-by-hole-or-ramp-down-by-track-or-tunnel.md)). Up is either elevator hole or the right-hand ascending ramp; down is the right-hand
return track or the raised tunnel on the left. Pages:
[the two levels](../concepts/the-two-levels.md).

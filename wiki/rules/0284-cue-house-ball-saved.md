# Rule 0284 — `HOUSE_BALL_SAVED`

**At the glass: the machine telling you it caught the ball** — it fires instead of `BALL_LOST`,
once a turn, and nothing is counted or cleared ([0284](../../raw/rules/0284-cue-house-ball-saved.md)). Pages: [what the machine says](../concepts/the-sounds.md), [drain protection](../concepts/drain-protection.md).

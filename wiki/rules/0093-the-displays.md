# Rule 0093 — What each display shows during a game

**At the glass: four scores, a turn score, a count of turns left, a count of extra balls, a winking
eye, and two digits that are painted over** ([0093](../../raw/rules/0093-the-displays.md)). Pages:
[what the glass shows](../concepts/what-the-glass-shows.md), [the `EXTRA PLAYS` eye](../things/extra-plays-eye.md).

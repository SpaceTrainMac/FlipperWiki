# The knocker

**Three hits of the knocker is Space Train Bonus** — the machine's loudest announcement, for all
three multipliers reaching ×3 ([0043](../../raw/rules/0043-space-ship-bonus-and-space-train-bonus-are.md)).

**One hit during a transfer means you have just taken first place.** It fires at the tick where
your total passes the standing record — first place only, once a player a game ([0072](../../raw/rules/0072-the-knocker-fires-during-the-transfer.md)). So the
knocker says two things: this, and Space Train Bonus ([0072](../../raw/rules/0072-the-knocker-fires-during-the-transfer.md)). See
[Space Ship Bonus and Space Train Bonus](../concepts/flyer-bonuses.md).

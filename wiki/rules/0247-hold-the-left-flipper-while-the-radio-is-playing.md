# Rule 0247 — Hold the left flipper while the radio is playing

**At the glass: hold the left flipper and the radio's level climbs; let go and that is the volume,
saved** ([0247](../../raw/rules/0247-hold-the-left-flipper-while-the-radio-is-playing.md)). It exists as a gesture rather than a menu entry because a volume is judged by ear
and the service menu makes no sound ([0247](../../raw/rules/0247-hold-the-left-flipper-while-the-radio-is-playing.md)). Pages: [the cabinet](../bodies/cabinet.md).

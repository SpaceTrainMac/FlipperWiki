[cabinet.md § 2. The service menu - The radio, and the three buttons in it](../../../FlipperEngine/docs/cabinet.md#the-radio-and-the-three-buttons-in-it)

**A tap is under a second, a volume is over it.** Let go before the number appears and you are back
in attract mode; let go after it and you have set a volume and stayed. Reaching for both flippers
while the number is up abandons it — the volume stays where it was and the service menu opens as
usual.

**`CREDITS` counts extra balls because this cabinet has no credits to count.** The legend is wrong
and the field is the right width, and what a player wants under it is the same thing a credit used
to mean: *how much more do I get to play*. One up for every extra ball awarded, one down for every
ball that drains, and **the turn ends when a ball drains with that number at zero**.

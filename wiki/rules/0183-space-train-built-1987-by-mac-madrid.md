# Rule 0183 — SPACE TRAIN, built 1987 by MAC of Madrid

**At the glass: two printed markings are the machine's own signature** — `© 1987 MAC, S.A. ALL
RIGHT RESERVED` on the apron, and `SPACE TRAIN MAC / SYSTEM IV / A-01717` stencilled on the relay
board inside the backbox ([0183](../../raw/rules/0183-space-train-built-1987-by-mac-madrid.md)). Pages: [the 1987 machine](../bodies/1987.md).

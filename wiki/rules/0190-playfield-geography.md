# Rule 0190 — Playfield geography

**At the glass: the ramps and tunnels have names, and one 1987 drawing is where they come from.**
FLYER02 labels four routes bilingually — the elevator holes, the ascending ramp, the return track
and the raised tunnel — and no other source names any of them ([0190](../../raw/rules/0190-playfield-geography.md)). Pages:
[the two levels](../concepts/the-two-levels.md).

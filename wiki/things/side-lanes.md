# The side lanes

**Three switches a side, and the playfield prints what each one pays** ([0048](../../raw/rules/0048-three-switches-a-side-and-the-playfield-prints.md)):

| Lane | Printed | Pays |
|---|---|---|
| the outer of the three | `500` | **500** |
| [the inlane](inlanes-5000.md) | `5000` | **5 000**, and five bonuses |
| [the outlane](outlanes.md) | `SPECIAL` | **5 000** when `SPECIAL` is lit, and both lamps go out. Nothing when it is dark |

*(the table is rule 0048's, rendered for a player)*

**Which switch is which lane was decided, not documented** ([0212](../../raw/rules/0212-d4-the-three-sidelane-switches-are-the-printed-values.md)). Three printed values and
exactly three switches a side is the whole of the evidence; one switch was already known to pay
5 000, and that fixes the remaining two in the only order that fits — so the table above is an
[argument from counting](../concepts/where-the-sources-are-silent.md) rather than a reading
([0212](../../raw/rules/0212-d4-the-three-sidelane-switches-are-the-printed-values.md)).

**The three words are painted on the lanes themselves** — `SPECIAL`, `5000` and `500` on the
outlanes and inlanes — so *1987* tells you what each pays before you ever hit one ([0196](../../raw/rules/0196-lower-playfield-foto05.md)).

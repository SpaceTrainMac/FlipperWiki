# Every award, in one table

**The complete scoring reference.** Where a value is multiplied, the multiplier is that mechanism's
own — see [the three multipliers](the-multipliers.md).

| Award | Value | Paid |
|---|---|---|
| a pop bumper | **100** | at once |
| an `ADVANCE BONUS` target, first hit of the bank | **100** | at once |
| completing the four-target bank | **10 000** + one bonus + the combination stage | at once |

**Lower playfield (FOTO05).** The centre bonus ladder, positions `1`…`10`, labelled vertically
**`BONUS X 5000`** — the printed authority that **one bonus = 5 000 points**. **`SUPER BONUS`** up
the left with positions **`10`**, **`20`**, **`30`**. **`HOLE BONUS`** arrows on both left and right,
each beside a ladder of its own. `TRIPLE` and `DOUBLE` beside the saucer and again between the
flippers. An `X BONUS` target. Outlanes/inlanes printed `SPECIAL`, `5000` and `500`.

The three positions are the ones in [`tests/fixtures/sounds.json`](../../../FlipperEngine/tests/fixtures/sounds.json);
a cabinet's own playlist puts them wherever its operator did. **All three themes are also playlist
entries**, so a theme can be interrupted by nothing except itself coming round again.

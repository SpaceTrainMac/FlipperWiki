# Rule 0091 — The draw

**At the glass: watch the last two digits of your score. If the number that comes up matches them,
you get another ball** ([0091](../../raw/rules/0091-when-every-player-is-out-of-balls-the.md)). Pages: [the draw](../moments/the-draw.md),
[a ball, a turn and a game](../concepts/ball-turn-game.md).

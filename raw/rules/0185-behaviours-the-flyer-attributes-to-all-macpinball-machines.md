Behaviours the flyer attributes to *all* MacPinBall machines, i.e. properties of System IV rather
than of Space Train: **automatic percentaging** (the machine self-adjusts how freely it awards extra
balls and plays), **2 extra plays at high score**, an **Info-Speech** speaking announcer, and
**1–4 players**.

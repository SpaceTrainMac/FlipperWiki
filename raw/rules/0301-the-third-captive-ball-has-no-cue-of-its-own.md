[sound.md § 4. The catalogue - Effects, The captive balls, hole bonus](../../../FlipperEngine/docs/sound.md#the-captive-balls--hole-bonus)

The card's third captive ball, `BALLWITHBALL_RIGHT_TOP`, drives no ladder — it arms both markers,
collects the right `EXTRA BALL`, takes the upper one, and takes the extra bonus. It is the busiest
switch on the machine and it has no cue of its own; what it raises is the cue of whatever it just
collected.

**One to four players, three balls each, taken in turn.**

| | |
|---|---|
| **A turn** | one ball served, played until no ball is in play and none is owed |
| **A ball** | from the plunger lane to the trough — several of them fit in one turn |
| **A game** | three turns per player, plus whatever the draw awards |

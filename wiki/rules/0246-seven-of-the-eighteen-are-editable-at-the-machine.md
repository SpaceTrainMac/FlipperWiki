# Rule 0246 — Seven of them are editable at the machine

**At the glass: volume, sound set, how effects and music pick a file, the radio station, how long
it waits before the radio starts, and how long the credits run** — seven things an owner can set
standing in front of the machine ([0246](../../raw/rules/0246-seven-of-the-eighteen-are-editable-at-the-machine.md)). The radio station has a second way in, the right flipper
while the radio plays ([0246](../../raw/rules/0246-seven-of-the-eighteen-are-editable-at-the-machine.md)). Pages: [the cabinet](../bodies/cabinet.md).

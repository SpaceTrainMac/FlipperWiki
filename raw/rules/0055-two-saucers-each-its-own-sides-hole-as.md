**Two saucers, each its own side's hole as well as its own other job.** They are the flyer's two
*elevator holes*: the only pair of holes on the machine that swallows a ball and shoots it out again
under coil power, which is what *2 shooter holes to upper level* describes.
[machine.md §1.4](../../../FlipperArchitecture/docs/machine.md#14-playfield-geography) puts them upper centre.

# Rule 0196 — Lower playfield (FOTO05)

**At the glass: almost everything the lower playfield does is written on it.** `BONUS X 5000` runs
down the centre ladder — the only *1987* statement of what one bonus is worth — `SUPER BONUS` up
the left with `10`, `20`, `30`; a `HOLE BONUS` arrow beside each of the two side ladders; `TRIPLE`
and `DOUBLE` beside the saucer and again between the flippers; and `SPECIAL`, `5000` and `500` on
the lanes ([0196](../../raw/rules/0196-lower-playfield-foto05.md)). Two things in the photograph do not line up with the matrix and are marked
**TBC** on their pages: the centre ladder's ten positions against nine lamps, and an `X BONUS`
*target* where the machine has only a lamp ([0196](../../raw/rules/0196-lower-playfield-foto05.md)). Pages:
[the bonus ladder](../things/bonus-ladder.md), [the DOUBLE and TRIPLE lamps](../things/multiplier-lamps.md),
[the X BONUS lamp](../things/x-bon-lamp.md), [the two hole-bonus ladders](../things/hole-bonus-ladders.md),
[the side lanes](../things/side-lanes.md).

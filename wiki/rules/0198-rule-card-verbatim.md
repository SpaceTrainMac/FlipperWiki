# Rule 0198 — Rule card — verbatim

**At the glass: the card on the right of the apron is the whole game in nine lines, and eight of
them still hold** ([0198](../../raw/rules/0198-rule-card-verbatim.md)). The exception is the first stage of the combination: the card gives it
the **upper** `EXTRA BALL`, which on this cabinet is
[M-A-C's `A`](../concepts/m-a-c.md) instead ([0198](../../raw/rules/0198-rule-card-verbatim.md)). Pages:
[the two cards on the apron](../things/apron-cards.md),
[the advance-bonus bank](../concepts/advance-bonus-bank.md).

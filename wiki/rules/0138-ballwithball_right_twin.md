# Rule 0138 — `BALLWITHBALL_RIGHT_TWIN`

**Pays:** —. **Advances:** the **right** hole ladder by one rung, or opens/redeems its 30 s window at the 5th/6th hit. **Collects:** the right ladder's five rungs and marker, if that side's window is open. ([0138](../../raw/rules/0138-ballwithball_right_twin.md))

It is explained on [centre captive balls](../things/centre-captive-balls.md).

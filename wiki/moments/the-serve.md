# The serve

**A ball leaves the store, arrives in the start ramp, and becomes a ball in play when it leaves the
ramp** ([0077](../../raw/rules/0077-a-ball-is-served-from-a-two-ball-store.md)). **You fire it yourself** — the machine fills the ramp, [the plunger](../things/plunger.md) empties
it, and there is no auto-launch ([0078](../../raw/rules/0078-a-ball-begins-in-the-start-ramp-and.md)). The three contacts that mark those steps are
[the ball store's](../things/ball-store.md).

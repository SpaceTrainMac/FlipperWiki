[gameplay.md § 8. The side lanes, the sidebars and the bumpers](../../../FlipperArchitecture/docs/gameplay.md#8-the-side-lanes-the-sidebars-and-the-bumpers)

**Each of the three pop bumpers pays 100 and flashes its own lamp for 300 ms** (configurable).
Simultaneous hits on two bumpers pay twice. There is no bumper multiplier and no bumper progression:
the bumpers are the machine's floor, not one of its mechanics.

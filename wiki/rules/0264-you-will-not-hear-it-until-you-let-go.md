# Rule 0264 — Silent while it climbs, because the ladder wraps

**At the glass: you are choosing by eye, not by ear** — the level is silent until you let go,
because a ladder that wrapped while audible would blast the room at 100 and then cut to silence
([0264](../../raw/rules/0264-you-will-not-hear-it-until-you-let-go.md)). Pages: [the radio](../moments/the-radio.md).

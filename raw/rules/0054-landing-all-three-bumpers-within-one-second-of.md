[gameplay.md § 8. The side lanes, the sidebars and the bumpers](../../../FlipperArchitecture/docs/gameplay.md#8-the-side-lanes-the-sidebars-and-the-bumpers)

**Landing all three bumpers within one second of each other earns a thunderstrike.** The backlight
flashes white four times — two short flashes at 300 ms, two long flashes at 600 ms, about 300 ms
apart — and pays a flat **500** on top of the 300 the three bumpers have already paid on their own.
It rewards landing all three in a hurry, not a skill shot: nothing about it multiplies, nothing caps
how often it repeats in a ball, and it does not care which order the three bumpers came in, only
that all three closed inside the one second.

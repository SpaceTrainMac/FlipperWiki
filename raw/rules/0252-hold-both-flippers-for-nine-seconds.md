**Hold both flippers for nine seconds while the cabinet is in attract mode.** There is no service
button — every matrix input is a playfield switch or one of the three buttons — so the way in is a
gesture. A tap will not do it and one flipper will not do it, which is deliberate: both of those
happen to a cabinet all evening.

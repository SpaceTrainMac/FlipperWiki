# Rule 0185 — Behaviours the flyer attributes to all MacPinBall machines

**At the glass: automatic percentaging, 2 extra plays at high score, an Info-Speech announcer and
1–4 players are chassis features**, sold with every MAC machine rather than designed for this one
([0185](../../raw/rules/0185-behaviours-the-flyer-attributes-to-all-macpinball-machines.md)). Two of them left marks a player can still see — the `EXTRA PLAYS` legend on the backbox,
and the fact that the machine speaks at all ([0185](../../raw/rules/0185-behaviours-the-flyer-attributes-to-all-macpinball-machines.md)). Pages:
[the 1987 machine](../bodies/1987.md), [the EXTRA PLAYS eye](../things/extra-plays-eye.md),
[what the machine says](../concepts/the-sounds.md).

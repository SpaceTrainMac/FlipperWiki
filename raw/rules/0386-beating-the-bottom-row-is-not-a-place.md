Beating the bottom row is not a place; surviving the trim is. Those stop being the same question the
moment two players qualify in the same game: on a default table, four players on 1 100, 1 200, 1 300
and 1 400 each beat the bottom row, and fourteen rows trimmed to ten keep one of them.

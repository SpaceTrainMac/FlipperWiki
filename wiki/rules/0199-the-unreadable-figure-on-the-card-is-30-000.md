# Rule 0199 — `[?]0.000` is 30 000

**At the glass: the marker the card promises is worth 30 000, and the only thing that was ever in
doubt is a single digit in a photograph** ([0199](../../raw/rules/0199-the-unreadable-figure-on-the-card-is-30-000.md)). A 3 and a 5 look alike at 800 × 600, so the
figure was decided by three sources that do not depend on the photograph: the spreadsheet names the
lamps *Hole Bonus 30000*, the upper bank is printed `30.000`, and the engine awards `30000`
([0199](../../raw/rules/0199-the-unreadable-figure-on-the-card-is-30-000.md)). Pages: [the two cards on the apron](../things/apron-cards.md),
[the hole bonus](../concepts/the-hole-bonus.md).

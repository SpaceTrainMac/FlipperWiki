# Rule 0433 — Credits are unlimited

**At the glass: never look at `CREDITS` for a credit.** The coin slots are not used and the credits
are unlimited, so outside a game the legend means nothing — and during one the field holds
[extra balls](../things/status-row.md) instead ([0433](../../raw/rules/0433-credits-are-unlimited-and-mean-nothing-outside-a-game.md)). Pages:
[the status row](../things/status-row.md).

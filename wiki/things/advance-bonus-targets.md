# The four ADVANCE BONUS targets

**Four targets low on the playfield, and hitting all four is the machine's only three-stage award**
([0014](../../raw/rules/0014-four-advance-bonus-targets-on-the-lower-playfield.md)). They are the bank; what it is worth is
[the advance-bonus bank](../concepts/advance-bonus-bank.md).

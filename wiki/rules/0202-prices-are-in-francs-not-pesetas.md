# Rule 0202 — Prices are in francs, not pesetas

**At the glass: a Madrid-built machine with its prices in francs was working somewhere
French-speaking** ([0202](../../raw/rules/0202-prices-are-in-francs-not-pesetas.md)). It settles nothing about play, and it does put one caveat under
everything the cabinet arrived configured with: those settings may be a route operator's rather
than the factory's ([0202](../../raw/rules/0202-prices-are-in-francs-not-pesetas.md)). Pages: [the two cards on the apron](../things/apron-cards.md),
[the 1987 machine](../bodies/1987.md).

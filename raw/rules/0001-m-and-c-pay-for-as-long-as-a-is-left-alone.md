[gameplay.md § 3. M-A-C](../../../FlipperArchitecture/docs/gameplay.md#3-m-a-c)

**`M` and `C` pay for as long as the player leaves `A` alone.** Neither is spent by being taken, so
a player who can keep finding the two outer lanes keeps being paid 30 000 for each. **`A` is the
lane that closes the sequence**, which makes when to take it the only real decision M-A-C asks of a
player.

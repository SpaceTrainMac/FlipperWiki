# Rule 0211 — D3: `SPECIAL` is worth 5 000, and it is an outlane award

**At the glass: neither what `SPECIAL` pays nor where you collect it comes from 1987 paper.** The
lamps are named *Special 5000* on the machine's own spreadsheet, the outlanes are the lanes printed
`SPECIAL` and the ones carrying the switches, and with credits out of scope there is nothing else
it could pay ([0211](../../raw/rules/0211-d3-special-is-worth-5-000-and-is-an-outlane-award.md)). Pages: [the special](../concepts/special.md),
[decided where the sources are silent](../concepts/where-the-sources-are-silent.md).

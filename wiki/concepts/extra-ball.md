# The extra ball

**One way to an extra ball is M-A-C: take `A` with the three letters lit, then hit the right captive
ball inside 20 seconds.** The upper `EXTRA BALL` lamp and the `A` lamp flash for the window, which is
configurable; if it closes first, nothing is awarded
([0002](../../raw/rules/0002-a-opens-a-20-second-window-on-the-upper-extra-ball.md)). See
[M-A-C](m-a-c.md), [the upper target bank](../things/upper-target-bank.md) and
[the right captive ball](../things/right-captive-ball.md).

# Rule 0436 — `ALL SETS` pools the five into one cabinet

**At the glass: a bumper can answer in chiptune, then in an orchestra, then in a voice, then from a
band** — every cue draws from every set that maps it, the playlists are concatenated, and the
jukebox has forty pieces in it ([0436](../../raw/rules/0436-all-sets-pools-the-five-into-one-cabinet.md)). The one thing to expect from it is uneven
volume: one mixer, no ducking, and five sets mastered separately ([0436](../../raw/rules/0436-all-sets-pools-the-five-into-one-cabinet.md)). It supersedes
[0270](../../raw/rules/0270-all-sets-pools-the-four-into-one-cabinet.md), which pooled four.

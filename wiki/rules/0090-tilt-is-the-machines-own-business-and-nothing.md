# Rule 0090 — A tilt does not void your bonus

**At the glass: a tilt kills the cabinet's outputs until the ball is home, and then the bonus is
counted as usual. You lose the flippers and the lamps, not the score** ([0090](../../raw/rules/0090-tilt-is-the-machines-own-business-and-nothing.md)). Pages:
[tilt](../concepts/tilt.md), [the end of a ball](../moments/end-of-ball.md).

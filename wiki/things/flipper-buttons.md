# The flipper buttons

**Beside flipping, the buttons do one thing: they rotate the three M-A-C letters one position.**
Left or right, all three letters move — the lane-change idiom, which is why a letter rolled by
accident is not simply lost ([0004](../../raw/rules/0004-spelling-it.md)). The letters are
[the M, A and C lanes](m-a-c-lanes.md)'; the mechanic is [M-A-C](../concepts/m-a-c.md).

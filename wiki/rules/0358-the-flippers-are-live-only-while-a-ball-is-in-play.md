# Rule 0358 — The flippers are live for exactly as long as a ball is on the playfield

**At the glass: dead flippers between turns are the machine working** ([0358](../../raw/rules/0358-the-flippers-are-live-only-while-a-ball-is-in-play.md)). The longest stretch
is the three seconds the draw number stands on the glass ([0358](../../raw/rules/0358-the-flippers-are-live-only-while-a-ball-is-in-play.md)). Pages: [the flipper buttons](../things/flipper-buttons.md), [the draw](../moments/the-draw.md).

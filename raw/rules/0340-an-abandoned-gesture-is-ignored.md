**An abandoned gesture is ignored rather than acted on**, and this is true of both of them. The joke
goes up at three seconds; if the buttons are released before the nine are up, the sequences start
moving again but the text is *left on the glass* until the end of the next up or down run. Snatching it off at the
instant of release takes it away from somebody who is still reading it, which is the whole reason
there is a warning six seconds before anything happens at all.

# Rule 0068 — When a ball counts, and when it does not

**At the glass: a drain counts, an extra ball counts the ball before it, drain protection does not
count at all, and switching the machine off loses nothing** ([0068](../../raw/rules/0068-when-a-ball-counts-and-when-it-does.md)). Pages:
[the end of a ball](../moments/end-of-ball.md), [the `HOUSE BALL` lamp](../things/house-ball-lamp.md).

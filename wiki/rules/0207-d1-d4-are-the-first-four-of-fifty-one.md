# Rule 0207 — D1–D4 are the first four of fifty-one

**At the glass: the four decisions listed with the 1987 sources are the oldest four of
fifty-one**, and everything decided after them — every switch, every lamp, the end of a ball, a
turn and a game — has been answered since ([0207](../../raw/rules/0207-d1-d4-are-the-first-four-of-fifty-one.md)). A reader who stops at that list comes away
thinking the machine is far more undecided than it is ([0207](../../raw/rules/0207-d1-d4-are-the-first-four-of-fifty-one.md)). Pages:
[decided where the sources are silent](../concepts/where-the-sources-are-silent.md).

# Rule 0230 — Multiball is two balls, not the flyer's three

**At the glass: two balls is right and the sales flyer is not** ([0230](../../raw/rules/0230-multiball-is-two-balls-not-the-flyers-three.md)). The trough holds two and
the left bunker starts multiball only when one is cached, so a third was never available on this
cabinet whatever MAC printed ([0230](../../raw/rules/0230-multiball-is-two-balls-not-the-flyers-three.md)). Pages: [multiball](../concepts/multiball.md),
[the Visual Pinball table](../bodies/vpx.md).

[gameplay.md § 6. The hole bonuses, and the captive balls](../../../FlipperArchitecture/docs/gameplay.md#6-the-hole-bonuses-and-the-captive-balls)

**The right captive ball lights both markers, with no timer of its own.** It collects the right
`EXTRA BALL` if it is lit, and it advances no ladder — it is the card's *RIGHT BALL LIGHTS 30.000 ON
CENTER BALLS*, a shot that arms both centre balls' markers without any of the five hits that would
otherwise be needed. A marker armed this way sits steadily lit — it does not blink and it does not
expire — until its own side's ladder next reaches its fifth hit, or the ball ends.

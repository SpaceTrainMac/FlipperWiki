- **Bonus forfeit.** Real machines usually void the ball's bonus on tilt. Since the host sees a
  normal drain, it will score it as one. Whether that is the wanted behaviour is a rules question and
  belongs in [gameplay.md](../../../FlipperArchitecture/docs/gameplay.md).

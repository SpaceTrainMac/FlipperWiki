# Rule 0313 — `SPACE_TRAIN_BONUS`

**At the glass: the most expensive thing on the machine, and it announces itself accordingly**
([0313](../../raw/rules/0313-cue-space-train-bonus.md)). A steam train starting and flying away, `SPACE TRAIN LIFTS OFF` across the four player
displays for five seconds, and three hits of [the knocker](../things/knocker.md) ([0313](../../raw/rules/0313-cue-space-train-bonus.md)). Pages: [what the machine says](../concepts/the-sounds.md), [the Space Train banner](../moments/the-space-train-banner.md).

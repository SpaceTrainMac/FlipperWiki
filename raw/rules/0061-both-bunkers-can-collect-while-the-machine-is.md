**Both bunkers can collect while the machine is still live.** During multiball another ball is on
the playfield the whole time a marker is being counted out, and the rest of the machine keeps
scoring underneath it — the freeze that [§10](../../../FlipperArchitecture/docs/gameplay.md#10-the-end-of-a-ball-the-count) describes applies to
the end of a ball and not here.

# Rule 0010 — The letters carry from ball to ball for the whole game

**At the glass: draining does not cost you your letters — the next ball starts with whatever was
lit, and three lit letters start it complete. What a drain does take is an open `EXTRA BALL` window**
([0010](../../raw/rules/0010-the-letters-carry-from-ball-to-ball-for.md)). Pages: [M-A-C](../concepts/m-a-c.md), [the M, A and C lanes](../things/m-a-c-lanes.md),
[the end of a ball](../moments/end-of-ball.md).

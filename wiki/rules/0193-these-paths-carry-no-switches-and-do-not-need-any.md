# Rule 0193 — These paths carry no switches, and they do not need any

**At the glass: riding a ramp or a tunnel scores nothing, and the machine never asks which level
the ball is on** ([0193](../../raw/rules/0193-these-paths-carry-no-switches-and-do-not-need-any.md)). Every scoring element has a switch of its own, so the level falls out of
whatever closed; the two storeys are something to look at rather than state the machine tracks
([0193](../../raw/rules/0193-these-paths-carry-no-switches-and-do-not-need-any.md)). Pages: [the two levels](../concepts/the-two-levels.md).

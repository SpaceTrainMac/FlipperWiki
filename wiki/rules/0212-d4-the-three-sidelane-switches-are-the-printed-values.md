# Rule 0212 — D4: the three side-lane switches are the three printed values

**At the glass: the lanes pay what they say, and working out which switch was which took counting
rather than a source.** `SPECIAL`, `5000` and `500` are printed on the lanes, there are exactly
three switches a side, and one of them was already known to pay 5 000 — leaving the other two in
the only arrangement that fits ([0212](../../raw/rules/0212-d4-the-three-sidelane-switches-are-the-printed-values.md)). Pages: [the side lanes](../things/side-lanes.md),
[decided where the sources are silent](../concepts/where-the-sources-are-silent.md).

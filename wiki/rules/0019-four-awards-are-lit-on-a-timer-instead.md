# Rule 0019 — Four awards are lit on a timer instead

**At the glass: four things do not wait for you — the upper `EXTRA BALL` for 20 seconds, the extra
bonus for 20, a hole-bonus redemption for 30, drain protection for 15. Miss the window and it pays
nothing at all** ([0019](../../raw/rules/0019-four-awards-are-lit-on-a-timer-instead.md)). Pages: [lit awards](../concepts/lit-awards.md),
[the `X BONUS` lamp](../things/x-bon-lamp.md), [the `HOUSE BALL` lamp](../things/house-ball-lamp.md),
[the two hole-bonus ladders](../things/hole-bonus-ladders.md),
[the upper target bank](../things/upper-target-bank.md).

# The DOUBLE and TRIPLE lamps

**Three pairs, and each pair belongs to one collection.** `X2_BOT` and `X3_BOT` at the bottom are
[the bonus count](../concepts/the-bonus-count.md)'s; a pair beside each hole ladder is that side's
([0038](../../raw/rules/0038-three-independent-pairs-of-double-triple-lamps-and.md)). What each is earned by is [the three multipliers](../concepts/the-multipliers.md).

**On the playfield they are printed twice over, and in two places**: a `TRIPLE` / `DOUBLE` pair
beside the saucer, and another between the flippers ([0196](../../raw/rules/0196-lower-playfield-foto05.md)). The *1987* photograph shows both, and
a player looking for the bottom pair looks between the flippers ([0196](../../raw/rules/0196-lower-playfield-foto05.md)).

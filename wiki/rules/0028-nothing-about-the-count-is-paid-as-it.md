# Rule 0028 — Nothing about the count is paid as it climbs

**At the glass: the target's 100 and the inlane's 5 000 land on the `MATCH` display straight away —
it is only the bonus behind them that waits for the ball to end** ([0028](../../raw/rules/0028-nothing-about-the-count-is-paid-as-it.md)). Pages:
[the bonus count](../concepts/the-bonus-count.md).

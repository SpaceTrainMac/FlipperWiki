# The special

**Two things light `SPECIAL`: completing M-A-C, and the third completion of the `ADVANCE BONUS`
bank.** All three letters lit at once lights it on both outlanes ([0005](../../raw/rules/0005-completing-it.md)); so does the bank's third
completion, which also awards an extra ball outright ([0016](../../raw/rules/0016-the-fourth-target-completes-the-bank.md)). TBC — what it pays and how it is collected are not yet
rules here. See [the SPECIAL lamps](../things/special-lamps.md) and [M-A-C](m-a-c.md).

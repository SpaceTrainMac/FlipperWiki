# Rule 0299 — `TOPLANES_XTRABALL`

**At the glass: it speaks where the award is taken, not where it was opened** ([0299](../../raw/rules/0299-cue-toplanes-xtraball.md)). `A` opens
[a 20-second window](../things/upper-target-bank.md) and the extra ball is collected at the right
captive ball inside it; a window that closes uncollected pays nothing and says nothing ([0299](../../raw/rules/0299-cue-toplanes-xtraball.md)). Pages: [what the machine says](../concepts/the-sounds.md), [the upper target bank](../things/upper-target-bank.md).

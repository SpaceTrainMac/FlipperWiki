# Rule 0266 — Every single-flipper answer needs the other button up

**At the glass: two hands never do a one-handed thing.** A single-flipper answer only counts while
the other button is up, so reaching for both steps no station, moves no volume and drops you out of
nothing ([0266](../../raw/rules/0266-the-single-flipper-answers-need-the-other-button-up.md)). Pages: [the radio](../moments/the-radio.md).

[gameplay.md § 11. The end of a turn: the transfer](../../../FlipperArchitecture/docs/gameplay.md#11-the-end-of-a-turn-the-transfer)

**The knocker fires during the transfer, at the tick where the climbing total passes the standing
record** — first place only, once per player per game, and nothing at all for entering the table
lower down. **It is one of the two things the knocker says**: the other is Space Train Bonus, three
hits at the instant the third multiplier reaches ×3 ([§7](../../../FlipperArchitecture/docs/gameplay.md#the-two-the-flyer-names)).

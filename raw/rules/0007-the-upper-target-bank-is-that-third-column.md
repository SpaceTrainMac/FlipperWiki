**The upper target bank is that third column, and it has no switches of its own.** `M`, `A` and `C`
are the only contacts on the upper playfield; once M-A-C is complete they read which channel the
next ball takes, and each lights the lamp the playfield has an arrow painted to. The bank printed
`30.000 — EXTRA BALL — 30.000` is therefore the *display* of these three awards and never had
targets behind it.

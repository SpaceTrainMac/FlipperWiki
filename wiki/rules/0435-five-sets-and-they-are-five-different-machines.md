# Rule 0435 — There are five of them, and they are five different machines

**At the glass: an arcade in 1987, pop culture at full volume, a full orchestra, a cabinet that
talks to you, or a band with a singer in front of it** ([0435](../../raw/rules/0435-five-sets-and-they-are-five-different-machines.md)). All five answer the same forty
effects and the same three music cues, so the game is identical and the machine is not — and a set
missing an entry plays nothing there rather than failing ([0435](../../raw/rules/0435-five-sets-and-they-are-five-different-machines.md)). **The fifth one cannot be
built by a clone on its own**: its singing needs a voicebank somebody has to fetch and accept the
terms of, which no other set on this machine asks for ([0435](../../raw/rules/0435-five-sets-and-they-are-five-different-machines.md)). It supersedes
[0268](../../raw/rules/0268-four-sets-and-they-are-four-different-machines.md), which counted four.

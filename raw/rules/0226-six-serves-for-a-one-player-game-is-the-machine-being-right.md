**Six serves for a one-player game is the machine being right**: any drain within fifteen seconds of
`OUTFIRE` is protected and hands the ball back, and this driver's ball lives about two. Pass
`-b 16000` to play past that window and see ordinary turn boundaries, at seven times the wall clock.

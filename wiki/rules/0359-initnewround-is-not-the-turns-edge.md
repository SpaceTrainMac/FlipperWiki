# Rule 0359 — What happens at each edge of a turn

**At the glass: `BALL IN PLAY` is what separates a new turn from a new ball** ([0359](../../raw/rules/0359-initnewround-is-not-the-turns-edge.md)). Both reset
the playfield and serve a ball; only one of them counts ([0359](../../raw/rules/0359-initnewround-is-not-the-turns-edge.md)). Pages: [the end of a turn](../moments/end-of-turn.md), [the status row](../things/status-row.md).

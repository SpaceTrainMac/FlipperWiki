# The four player displays

**One six-digit display a player, and it holds your score for the whole game** ([0011](../../raw/rules/0011-ten-quantities-and-everything-below-is-one-of.md)). A score runs
to 9 999 999 — seven digits on a six-digit display, which is [§13](../../raw/rules/0011-ten-quantities-and-everything-below-is-one-of.md)'s problem
and not yet a rule here.

TBC — what happens past 999 999, and how the display behaves between turns, are §13's and §11's.
See [what the machine keeps track of](../concepts/what-the-machine-tracks.md).

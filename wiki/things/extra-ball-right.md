# The right EXTRA BALL lamp

**It lights on the second completion of the `ADVANCE BONUS` bank** ([0016](../../raw/rules/0016-the-fourth-target-completes-the-bank.md)). It is a different lamp
from [the upper `EXTRA BALL`](upper-target-bank.md), which M-A-C's `A` lane flashes for 20 seconds.

**It stays lit until you collect it at the right captive ball**, or until the ball ends — there is
no timer on this one ([0018](../../raw/rules/0018-the-lit-awards-wait-to-be-collected-which.md)). See
[the extra ball](../concepts/extra-ball.md) and
[the advance-bonus bank](../concepts/advance-bonus-bank.md).

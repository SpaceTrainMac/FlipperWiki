# Rule 0263 — The volume climbs in fives, one step a second, and wraps

**At the glass: hold the left flipper and the number climbs in fives, one a second, wrapping from
100 to 0** — so one button reaches every level and overshooting costs a lap ([0263](../../raw/rules/0263-the-volume-goes-up-in-fives-and-wraps.md)). Twenty-one
seconds is the whole way round ([0263](../../raw/rules/0263-the-volume-goes-up-in-fives-and-wraps.md)). Pages: [the radio](../moments/the-radio.md).

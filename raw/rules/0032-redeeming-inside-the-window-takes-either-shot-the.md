**Redeeming inside the window takes either shot the card names for this side**: the *corresponding*
bunker — `BUNKER_LEFT` for the left ladder, `BUNKER_RIGHT` for the right — or the same twin hit
again. Either pays the five rungs and the marker together, at that side's current multiplier, and
resets the ladder to climb from zero.

# The Space Train banner

**`SPACE TRAIN LIFTS OFF` is a still message across the four player displays, and it is the only
thing on this machine that writes them while a ball is in play** ([0101](../../raw/rules/0101-space-train-lifts-off-is-a-still-message.md)). The four are a 2×2 block
read as two rows, so the phrase lands one word to a display and nothing has to scroll ([0101](../../raw/rules/0101-space-train-lifts-off-is-a-still-message.md)):

```text
   SPACE    TRAIN          player 1, player 2
   LIFTS    OFF            player 3, player 4
   X-PLAYS BALL CREDITS      MATCH — the turn score, still counting
```

What earns it is [Space Train Bonus](../concepts/flyer-bonuses.md).

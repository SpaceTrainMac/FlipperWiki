# Rule 0092 — The count is per ball, the transfer is per turn

**At the glass: each ball is counted out as it ends, and the whole turn is handed over once, at the
end of it** ([0092](../../raw/rules/0092-the-count-is-per-ball-and-the.md)). Pages: [the end of a turn](../moments/end-of-turn.md),

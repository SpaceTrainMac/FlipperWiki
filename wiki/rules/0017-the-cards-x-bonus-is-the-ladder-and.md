# Rule 0017 — The card's x BONUS is the ladder, not a multiplier

**At the glass: `X BONUS` printed down the side of the bottom ladder is the name of the ladder.
Completing the bank lights one more of its lamps — it does not multiply anything** ([0017](../../raw/rules/0017-the-cards-x-bonus-is-the-ladder-and.md)). Pages:

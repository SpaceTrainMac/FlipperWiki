# Rule 0355 — Credits and coin handling are not implemented and not planned

**At the glass: this machine has four addresses for credits and no credits** — and the *credits*
at the end of a game are a roll of names, not a coin count ([0355](../../raw/rules/0355-credits-and-coin-handling-are-not-implemented.md)). Pages: [player select](../moments/player-select.md), [the status row](../things/status-row.md).

# Rule 0259 — Two words on the `MATCH` display mean something is wrong

**At the glass: `REBOOT` means the sound set you picked is not the one playing and will not be
until a restart; `NOSAVE` means a write failed and the old file is still there** ([0259](../../raw/rules/0259-two-words-on-the-match-display-mean-something-is-wrong.md)). Pages:
[the service menu](../moments/the-service-menu.md), [the MATCH display](../things/match-display.md).

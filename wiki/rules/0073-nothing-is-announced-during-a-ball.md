# Rule 0073 — Nothing is announced during a ball

**At the glass: you will not hear that you have taken the lead until your ball is over. Nothing
interrupts play** ([0073](../../raw/rules/0073-nothing-is-announced-during-a-ball.md)). Pages: [the end of a turn](../moments/end-of-turn.md).

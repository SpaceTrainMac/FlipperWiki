# Rule 0002 — A opens a 20-second window on the upper EXTRA BALL lamp

**At the glass: when the upper `EXTRA BALL` and the `A` lamp flash together, you have 20 seconds to
hit the right captive ball for an extra ball.** Miss the window and nothing is awarded
([0002](../../raw/rules/0002-a-opens-a-20-second-window-on-the-upper-extra-ball.md)). Pages:
[M-A-C](../concepts/m-a-c.md), [the extra ball](../concepts/extra-ball.md),
[the upper target bank](../things/upper-target-bank.md),
[the right captive ball](../things/right-captive-ball.md).

# Rule 0233 — The backbox is drawn, not photographed

**At the glass: the backbox is wider than the real one, and on purpose** ([0233](../../raw/rules/0233-the-backbox-is-drawn-not-photographed.md)). At the
cabinet's own proportions a six-digit score comes out about 19 pixels tall on a screen, so the
panel is redrawn rather than photographed and given room; the speaker grilles did not survive the
trade ([0233](../../raw/rules/0233-the-backbox-is-drawn-not-photographed.md)). Pages: [the Visual Pinball table](../bodies/vpx.md),
[what the glass shows](../concepts/what-the-glass-shows.md).

# Rule 0108 — The sixteen indicator LEDs

**At the glass: sixteen small lamps around the displays, and two of them can never light on this
cabinet — `TILT`, which has no switch behind it, and `CREDITS`, which has nothing to count**
([0108](../../raw/rules/0108-the-indicator-leds.md)). Pages: [the sixteen indicator LEDs](../things/indicator-leds.md).

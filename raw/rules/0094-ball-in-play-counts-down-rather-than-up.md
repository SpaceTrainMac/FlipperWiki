**`BALL IN PLAY` counts down rather than up**, and the number beside that legend is therefore not
the ball in play. It is the more useful of the two — *how many turns have I got left* is the
question a player actually asks — and the tension with the printed legend is deliberate and worth
knowing about. **It does not count extra balls**: those extend a turn without being a turn, and they
have a field of their own.

# Rule 0209 — D1: one bonus step is 5 000, on both hole-bonus ladders

**At the glass: the two hole ladders are worth the same, and they have not always been.** The left
one paid 1 000 until this was settled on 2026-08-26; no 1987 source gives its value, and the
argument that decided it is symmetry — same twin, same five rungs, same 30 000, so the same 5 000
([0209](../../raw/rules/0209-d1-one-bonus-step-is-5-000-on-both-ladders.md)). Pages: [the hole bonus](../concepts/the-hole-bonus.md),
[decided where the sources are silent](../concepts/where-the-sources-are-silent.md).

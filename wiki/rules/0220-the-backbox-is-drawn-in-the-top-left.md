# Rule 0220 — The backbox is drawn in the top left of the playfield window

**At the glass: look up-left, not at a second window** ([0220](../../raw/rules/0220-the-backbox-is-drawn-in-the-top-left.md)). A separate backglass window opens
behind a full-screen playfield and cannot be seen, so the table sizes the playfield to leave a gap
and draws the backbox into it ([0220](../../raw/rules/0220-the-backbox-is-drawn-in-the-top-left.md)). Pages: [the Visual Pinball table](../bodies/vpx.md).

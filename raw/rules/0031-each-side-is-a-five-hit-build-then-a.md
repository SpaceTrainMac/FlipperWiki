**Each side is a five-hit build, then a timed all-or-nothing redemption:**

| Hit on that side's twin | What happens |
|---|---|
| 1 – 4 | that rung's lamp lights |
| **5** | the fifth rung lamp lights, that side's `HOLE BONUS 30000` lamp lights with it, and all six lamps start blinking together: a configurable **30-second window** to redeem the whole thing |

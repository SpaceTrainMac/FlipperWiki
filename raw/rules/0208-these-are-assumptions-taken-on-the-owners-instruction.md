**These are assumptions, taken 2026-08-26 on the machine owner's instruction, and they are rules from
the moment they are written here.** The flyer, the card and the photographs do not answer them and
nobody alive is being asked; the instruction was *"make assumptions, you decide, it can be changed
later on."* So each one below says what was decided **and what it was decided from**, because the
reasoning is the part a future correction argues with. Overturning one is editing this section, not
finding a bug.

# Rule 0434 — `CREDITS` counts extra balls because this cabinet has no credits to count

**At the glass: the field under `CREDITS` is your extra balls, and outside a game the legend means
nothing at all** — the coin slots are not used and the credits are unlimited, so there is no
number to keep and nothing to run out of ([0434](../../raw/rules/0434-credits-counts-extra-balls-and-the-slots-are-unused.md)). **It supersedes
[0096](../../raw/rules/0096-credits-counts-extra-balls-because-this-cabinet-has.md)**, which was cut
from the same paragraph before it said why ([0434](../../raw/rules/0434-credits-counts-extra-balls-and-the-slots-are-unused.md)). Pages:
[the status row](../things/status-row.md), [the sixteen indicator LEDs](../things/indicator-leds.md).

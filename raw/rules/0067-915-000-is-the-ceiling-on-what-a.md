[gameplay.md § 10. The end of a ball: the count](../../../FlipperArchitecture/docs/gameplay.md#10-the-end-of-a-ball-the-count)

**915 000 is the ceiling on what a ball *collects*, and it is not the ceiling on what a ball is
worth.** Everything in the table above is paid when the ball ends; the awards paid *at once* are
uncapped, and the extra bonus alone is 500 000 of them. So a ball that fills the ladder and takes
the extra bonus is worth more than 1 400 000 on its own, and [§13](../../../FlipperArchitecture/docs/gameplay.md#past-a-million)'s `×10`
indicator is not a two-good-balls problem but a one-good-ball one.
